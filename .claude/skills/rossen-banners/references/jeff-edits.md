# Jeff's banner edits: two real shows

Each show below has the writer's draft, the list Jeff sent back, and the reason for
every change. Read the reasons, not just the lines. The goal is to write the way he
edits, not to copy these banners.

## Friday 9/25: phone scams + Amazon deals

Show: new-phone return scam and port-out phone hijacking, guest Jim Stickley
(ethical hacker), then Amazon deals with Nader from DealSeek.

**What aired, in air order:**

```
WELCOME TO ROSSEN REPORTS
THIS NEW PHONE SCAM STEALS YOUR NUMBER
SCAMMERS HIJACK YOUR PHONE & BANK ACCOUNT
LIKE AND SUBSCRIBE
JOIN THE CHAT
🔥 AMAZON'S HOTTEST DEALS
🔥 WE FOUND AMAZON'S SECRET PROMO CODES
JIM STICKLEY | ETHICAL HACKER
THIS "NEW PHONE" SCAM CAN DRAIN YOUR ACCOUNT
CHANGE THIS SETTING ON YOUR PHONE NOW
NADER MARCOS, DEALSEEK CO-FOUNDER
DEALSEEK.COM/ROSSEN
```

| Draft | Jeff | Why |
|---|---|---|
| NEW SCAM TARGETS YOUR NEW PHONE | THIS NEW PHONE SCAM STEALS YOUR NUMBER | "Targets" is a label. "Steals your number" is the harm. "THIS" makes you wait for it. |
| SCAMMERS HIJACK YOUR PHONE, BANK | SCAMMERS HIJACK YOUR PHONE & BANK ACCOUNT | "BANK" alone reads as a building. "BANK ACCOUNT" is your money. "&" keeps it short. |
| *amazon deal line with the emoji* | 🔥 WE FOUND AMAZON'S SECRET PROMO CODES | A placeholder Jeff had to fill. "WE FOUND" is the investigator's voice; "SECRET" is the hook the bible already had. 🔥 is the one emoji the show uses, on the Amazon tease only. |
| NEW PHONE? BEWARE "CARRIER" CALLS | *(cut)* | BEWARE is filler. The question-mark setup spends words before the point. |
| SCAMMERS: "SEND YOUR PHONE BACK" | *(cut)* | A fine device, but one banner too many for a short A story. |
| JIM STICKLEY \| ETHICAL HACKER | *(kept)* | Moved to where Jim is brought in. |
| "NEW PHONE" SCAM LEAVES YOU WITH BILL | THIS "NEW PHONE" SCAM CAN DRAIN YOUR ACCOUNT | "A bill" is small. "Drain your account" is the biggest true stake. CAN keeps it honest. |
| "PORT-OUT" FRAUD HIJACKS YOUR PHONE, BANK | *(cut)* | Jargon, and it repeats the second banner. |
| PHONE HIJACKING SCAM WILL COST YOU THOUSANDS | *(cut)* | Third version of the same idea. |
| PREVENT "PORT-OUT" FRAUD BEFORE IT HAPPENS! | CHANGE THIS SETTING ON YOUR PHONE NOW | A concept became an action. Jargon gone. "THIS SETTING" teases the payoff; "NOW" is the urgency. |
| AMAZON DEALS BANNERS + LABEL FOR NADER | NADER MARCOS, DEALSEEK CO-FOUNDER then DEALSEEK.COM/ROSSEN | A note to self, not a banner. Jeff had to ask for the last name. The DealSeek segment is always the host's fixed label, then DEALSEEK.COM/ROSSEN for the rest of it. No banners for individual deals. |

Draft: 13 lines, 3 of them unfinished. Aired: 12 lines, all finished.

## Wednesday 9/16: scam victims call-in show

Show: viewers call in with scam stories. Guest Amy Nofziger (AARP). Callers Mary
(lost almost $200,000 to a fake Wells Fargo exec) and Alan (jury duty scam, fake
cops demanding $8,000).

**Jeff's list (aired):**

```
WELCOME TO ROSSEN REPORTS
EXCLUSIVE: SCAM VICTIMS TELL ALL
SCAM HOTLINE: 866-9-ROSSEN
AMY NOFZIGER | AARP FRAUD VICTIM SUPPORT
IMPOSTER SCAMS EXPLODING RIGHT NOW
CALL AND SHARE YOUR SCAM STORY!
ON THE PHONE: MARY | SCAMMED FOR $200,000
FAKE WELLS FARGO EXEC "INVESTED" MARY'S MONEY
SCAMMERS USED STOLEN IDENTITY
POLICE: MONEY "PROBABLY WENT OVERSEAS"
SCAM "RECOVERY" SERVICES ROB VICTIMS, AGAIN
ON THE PHONE: ALAN | NEW SCAM VICTIM
FAKE COPS THREATEN JAIL
SCAMMERS: "DEPOSIT $8,000" RIGHT NOW
THEY SEND THIS ARREST WARRANT
JURY DUTY SCAMS SPREADING FAST
SCAMMERS WARN "DON'T TELL YOUR FAMILY"
```

Jeff left the recovery-services banner in but wrote beside it: *"I don't know what
this one means. Banners need to be as few words as possible, to the point, and
provocative to express our point quickly, and make people want to watch."* Treat it
as a failure. A version that passes would say what happens in plain words, e.g.
`THEN THEY SCAM YOU A SECOND TIME` (this rewrite is ours, not Jeff's).

| Draft | Jeff | Why |
|---|---|---|
| CALL OUR HOTLINE: 866-9-ROSSEN | SCAM HOTLINE: 866-9-ROSSEN | Fewer words, and it says what the hotline is for. |
| IMPOSTER SCAMS ON THE RISE | IMPOSTER SCAMS EXPLODING RIGHT NOW | "On the rise" is newsroom filler. Strong verb plus urgency. |
| ON THE PHONE: MARY \| OCALA, FLORIDA | ON THE PHONE: MARY \| SCAMMED FOR $200,000 | A city gives no reason to listen. The dollar figure does. |
| MARY LOST ALMOST $200K TO SCAMMER | *(cut)* | Now in her label. Saying it twice wastes a banner. |
| FAKE WELLS FARGO EXEC "INVESTS" MARY'S MONEY | FAKE WELLS FARGO EXEC "INVESTED" MARY'S MONEY | It happened to her, so past tense. |
| SCAMMERS STOLE REAL BANKING EXEC'S IDENTITY | SCAMMERS USED STOLEN IDENTITY | Shorter. The secondary detail goes. |
| OCALA POLICE: MONEY "PROBABLY WENT OVERSEAS" | POLICE: MONEY "PROBABLY WENT OVERSEAS" | Location dropped. The quote is the hook. |
| ON THE PHONE: ALAN \| HOUSTON, TEXAS | ON THE PHONE: ALAN \| NEW SCAM VICTIM | Again no city. With no dollar loss, say what he is. |
| ALAN SKIPS JURY DUTY—FAKE COPS SAY "JAIL TIME" | FAKE COPS THREATEN JAIL | Two ideas joined by a dash. Kept the scary one, four words. |
| SCAMMERS: "DEPOSIT $8K IN KIOSK" | SCAMMERS: "DEPOSIT $8,000" RIGHT NOW | Full dollar figure. "Kiosk" is a detail that needs explaining. "RIGHT NOW" is the pressure the scammers apply. |
| THEY SEND TERRIFYING FAKE ARREST WARRANT | THEY SEND THIS ARREST WARRANT | Dropped the adjectives telling you how to feel. "THIS" points at the warrant on screen. |
| ALAN BARELY ESCAPES $8K SCAM | *(cut)* | Gives away the ending. |

Kept word for word because they already worked: `WELCOME TO ROSSEN REPORTS`,
`EXCLUSIVE: SCAM VICTIMS TELL ALL`, `AMY NOFZIGER | AARP FRAUD VICTIM SUPPORT`,
`CALL AND SHARE YOUR SCAM STORY!`, `JURY DUTY SCAMS SPREADING FAST`, `SCAMMERS WARN
"DON'T TELL YOUR FAMILY"`.

## How the 9/25 bible became banners

The bible is the raw material. This is where each of Jeff's banners came from.

| Bible copy | Banner |
|---|---|
| Tease headline: TWO NEW PHONES SCAMS THAT WILL STEAL YOUR NUMBER…AND DRAIN YOUR BANK ACCOUNT | THIS NEW PHONE SCAM STEALS YOUR NUMBER / SCAMMERS HIJACK YOUR PHONE & BANK ACCOUNT |
| Tease: PLUS THE BIGGEST AMAZON DEALS RIGHT NOW … SECRET PROMO CODES | 🔥 AMAZON'S HOTTEST DEALS / 🔥 WE FOUND AMAZON'S SECRET PROMO CODES |
| HIT LIKE AND SUBSCRIBE / JOIN THE CHAT | LIKE AND SUBSCRIBE / JOIN THE CHAT |
| (((BRING IN JIM))) — "ONE OF THE BEST HACKERS IN THE WORLD" | JIM STICKLEY \| ETHICAL HACKER |
| Beat headers on the return-phone scam, carriers refusing to eat the cost, port-out draining your bank | THIS "NEW PHONE" SCAM CAN DRAIN YOUR ACCOUNT |
| (CLEAR BANNERS FOR AD) | *(nothing: banners are down during the sponsor)* |
| WE FIGURED OUT EXACTLY WHICH SETTING WILL PROTECT YOU … AT&T, VERIZON, T-MOBILE | CHANGE THIS SETTING ON YOUR PHONE NOW |
| ((BRING NADER IN)) | NADER MARCOS, DEALSEEK CO-FOUNDER, then DEALSEEK.COM/ROSSEN for the whole segment |

Notice what didn't become a banner: the Massachusetts woman, the Utah man, the
spoofing explainer, the dark web aside, the social-media ransom beat, the three
carrier walkthroughs. Detail belongs in Jeff's mouth. The banner carries the one
thing a passing viewer needs.
