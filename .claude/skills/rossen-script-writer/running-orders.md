## Wednesday: confirm the shape first

**Ask how many stories before you lay out a single page.** There is no default
count you can assume your way into. Three shapes air:

| Shape | When |
|---|---|
| **A+B** | The default. One long A story, one shorter B story. |
| **3–5 story rundown** | A variant. Still one dominant A story, everything after it lighter. |
| **Single-topic umbrella** | One subject carries the body in numbered sub-stories — `TOP 5 FACEBOOK SCAMS`. |

The whole-show budget is the same in all three — 18–22 pages, 1,700–2,300 body
words, 10–12 clip beats. Only the division changes. See `measurements.md`.

## Wednesday A+B — the default

```
**SHOW TITLE / DATE**

**A STORY HEADLINE**              <- tease, all caps, clipped, urgent
-4 to 6 dash lines
**—------------------------------------------------**
**B STORY HEADLINE**
-4 to 6 dash lines

HIT LIKE AND SUBSCRIBE
JOIN THE CHAT
                                  <- pipeline boundary. tease ends here.
**A STORY TITLE**
-hook lines
-mechanics, in order
**MID-STORY BEAT HEADER**         <- bold caps, turns the story
-setup lines carrying specifics
(((PLAY CLIP XXX HORIZONTAL)))
OUT:
... repeat 5-7 times ...
**HERE'S HOW TO PROTECT YOURSELF**
-flat run of dash lines

(((SPONSOR TEASE - KILL BANNERS)))
**I'M ALWAYS LOOKING FOR WAYS TO PROTECT YOU FROM SCAMS…**
-OUR SPONSOR TODAY DOES JUST THAT.
(((PLAY SPONSOR)))

**B STORY TITLE**                 <- full second segment, shorter than A
-hook, mechanics, beat headers, 4-6 clips
**HERE'S HOW TO PROTECT YOURSELF**

-END OF SHOW
```

## Wednesday rundown — 3 to 5 stories

Same tease architecture, one separator-delimited block per story, ordered
strongest first. The A story keeps its 40–55% share; every story after it is
lighter than the one before.

```
**A STORY HEADLINE**
-4 to 6 dash lines
**—------------------------------------------------**
**STORY 2 HEADLINE**
-4 to 6 dash lines
**—------------------------------------------------**
**STORY 3 HEADLINE**
... one block per story, up to five ...

HIT LIKE AND SUBSCRIBE
JOIN THE CHAT

**A STORY TITLE**                 <- full treatment, 5-7 clips
(((SPONSOR TEASE - KILL BANNERS)))
(((PLAY SPONSOR)))
**STORY 2 TITLE** ... **STORY 3 TITLE** ...   <- descending weight

-END OF SHOW
```

A trailing story with **zero clip beats** is normal. Write it graphics-and-Jeff
and say so; do not invent a beat to even out the rundown.

## Wednesday umbrella — one topic

One subject across the whole body, in numbered sub-stories. The tease sells the
count and the topic, not four separate headlines.

```
**TOP 5 FACEBOOK SCAMS**
-4 to 6 dash lines teasing the worst two or three of them
-ALL FIVE, COMING UP RIGHT NOW.

HIT LIKE AND SUBSCRIBE
JOIN THE CHAT

**SCAM #1 — TITLE**               <- the strongest one, A-story weight and clips
-hook, mechanics, clip beats
**HERE'S HOW TO PROTECT YOURSELF**
(((SPONSOR TEASE - KILL BANNERS)))
(((PLAY SPONSOR)))
**SCAM #2 — TITLE** ... **SCAM #5 — TITLE**   <- short and even, 0-2 clips each

-END OF SHOW
```

Counted sub-stories are numbered in the copy, because the number is the promise
the tease made. The strongest one goes first even though a countdown format
implies otherwise — he is not building suspense, he is holding an audience.

## Rules that govern all three

- **Never assume the story count.** Confirm it, then lay out.
- **The whole-show budget is fixed** at 18–22 pages, 1,700–2,300 body words,
  10–12 clip beats, whichever shape is running.
- **The A story is 40–55% of the body and about half the clips**, always.
- **Every story after A is lighter**, descending through the rundown.
- **Sponsor drops at a story boundary** — after the A story, or after B in a
  longer rundown, or between sub-stories on an umbrella show — never mid-story.
  Where the sponsor's product connects to the story just told, bridge into it
  thematically rather than generically.
- **No good-news closer is required and none should be manufactured.** The show
  can end on a threat story. A non-scam money-saver is a legitimate segment type
  judged by what it is, not by where it sits — it can lead, it can trail, it can
  be absent.
- **Never invent a beat, a graphic, or a victim to fill a slot.** Write what the
  material carries and flag the deficit as a producer cue.

## Friday running order

Content story is fully written to Wednesday standard. The deals half is not
scripted.

```
**DATE — CONTENT STORY TITLE**
-tease lines for the content story
________________________________________
-THE HOTTEST AMAZON DEALS OF THE WEEK WITH THE FOLKS FROM DEALSEEK
-deal count and headline discount
________________________________________
-PLUS, YOUR LIVE REQUESTS
-HIT LIKE AND SUBSCRIBE
-JOIN THE CHAT

[content story, full treatment, 0-4 clip beats]

(((SPONSOR TEASE - KILL BANNERS)))
(((PLAY SPONSOR)))

**NOW, ONTO MY FAVORITE PART OF FRIDAYS**
**-BRING IN [GUEST]-**
-I WANT TO BRING IN [GUEST], FROM DEALSEEK.COM
((QR CODE UP))                    <- two parens, not three

- PRODUCT NAME
- RETAIL PRICE: $X
- DEAL PRICE: $Y
- Z% OFF WITH THE PROMO CODE      <- or PRICE DROP, or SUB & SAVE

(((JEFF AND [GUEST] LIVE DEAL REQUESTS)))

-END OF SHOW-
```

Ask which deals expert is on — it varies by episode (Trey Donovan, Nader
Marcos). Ask for the deal list and prices if not supplied; **do not invent
products or prices.**

### The screen-share walkthrough

Three of the five Friday bibles in the library are built on one — the secret
coupon page, the Shop-with-Points unlink, the subscriptions check. It is a
recurring form and it has a shape. Zero clip beats is normal; the walkthrough
*is* the segment.

```
**SO HOW DO YOU FIND OUT IF THIS IS HAPPENING TO YOU?**

-HERE'S THE STEP BY STEP THAT I WANT ALL OF YOU TO DO WITH ME…RIGHT NOW.

(((JEFF SCREEN SHARE)))

-LOG INTO YOUR AMAZON ACCOUNT.

-CLICK ON "**ACCOUNT & LISTS**" — A LONG DROP DOWN MENU WILL APPEAR.

-CLICK ON "**MEMBERSHIPS & SUBSCRIPTIONS**".

-MAKE SURE YOU SELECT "**CURRENT SUBSCRIPTIONS**".

-LOOK CAREFULLY AT EVERY SUBSCRIPTION ON THE LIST.

(((END SCREENSHARE)))

-AND REMEMBER YOU CAN PAUSE AND REWIND THIS VIDEO.
```

Rules for it: every click named in the order it happens; the exact label in
quotes and bold, because he is reading it off the screen live; one action per
line; `-AND REMEMBER YOU CAN PAUSE AND REWIND THIS VIDEO` after any path longer
than four steps. If a step needs a still rather than a live share, use
`(((TAKE ... STILL)))`. Phone numbers and URLs get their own line so he can slow
down on them.
