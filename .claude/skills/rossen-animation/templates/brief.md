# Rossen animation brief (fill in and paste as the prompt)

Fill the [brackets], keep the block for your film type, delete the others. The defaults at the bottom apply unless you change
them. For a live-show tease, `brief-tease.md` is the fuller version.

---

Make a [type: scam explainer / JEFF'S RULES episode / WHAT WOULD YOU DO? episode / LIVE TODAY promo / quiz / deals roundup /
opener] about [the topic], [9:16], about [45] seconds, ending on [the closing card with YouTube, Instagram and Facebook].

**Title card** (first 1–2 s): the Rossen logo, "[TITLE]", and "[a short line, e.g. LIVE WEDNESDAY · 5 PM ET]".

**The story** (one sentence per part, with the stake and your source for any number):
- [part 1: the bait / the setup]
- [part 2: the trap / the turn]
- [part 3: the loss]
- [part 4: the fix / the payoff]

**Story spine** (one line per beat; name the visual gag): [e.g. "the hook snags the phone and yanks it out of frame"] …

**Suggested captions:** "[CAPTION 1]", "[CAPTION 2]", … (short, ALL CAPS)

**Characters:** [Jeff, the Scammer, the everyday man, …]. [Attached: a new character, a reference, not a frame; animate as a
jointed paper-cutout puppet; the [prop] is a separate piece.]

**Transitions:** [one or two] creative ones caused by a character or prop ([idea]); the rest simple.

**Audio:** [the mood per part]. [A music mix / a voiceover bed Jeff will narrate over, with a timed VO script.]

### Type blocks (keep yours)

- **Scam explainer**: the fix is the payoff, held two bars ("[DON'T CLICK. GO TO THE APP YOURSELF.]"). The evidence Jeff marks
  up: [the text / email / page]. One sourced number: [the stat + source], or none.
- **JEFF'S RULES #[N]**: the rule "[RULE, two short lines]", the recap line "[…]", scenes before and after the rule: [list].
  Recurring parts stay as in earlier episodes.
- **WHAT WOULD YOU DO? #[N]**: the setup, the three options A/B/C (wrong, close, right) with what happens after each, the
  takeaway and a bonus tip. The right answer is revealed last.
- **LIVE TODAY promo**: the show's day and time, the one danger to tease, and the tell Jeff's magnifier finds.
- **Quiz**: [N] rounds, each a message with its answer (SCAM / LEGIT), the flags to mark and a takeaway line.
- **Deals roundup**: `intro/deals/deals.json` holds everything (names, exact prices, codes, price-check date and time,
  disclosure, credit, CTA per platform); product photos attached and cleared for use.
- **Opener**: the show's name and what it promises; 16:9 and/or 9:16.

---

Defaults (keep unless you change them):

- The approved "case file" screen-print style and palette; one shot, fun and charming with an investigative edge.
- Reuse the kits, puppets, both logos and the sample packs. If an image or asset is missing, stop and tell me. No substitutes.
- Captions short, bold, ALL CAPS, readable on a phone with the sound off; level; still long enough to read twice; one at a time;
  nothing moves across the words.
- 96 BPM, one idea per bar, whole bars, equal parts get equal bars, landings about 0.14 s early, sound effects on their attack.
- 9:16 safe zone: text and key action clear of the top 15 %, bottom 25 % and right 15 %; scenery may run off the edges.
- An original score in code with warm, real instruments; recorded CC0 or commercial-use sound effects, each logged in
  audio_sources.txt; say which sounds are composed.
- No real brands (except plain-text names the story needs), no real phone numbers or URLs, no real people except Jeff;
  obvious placeholders (JOHN DOE, 1-555-XXX-XXXX, ABC-0000); numbers only from a source.
- Show me the bar plan and the captions before building.
- Verify on the final mp4 (sync within about 10 ms, every caption readable for its time, the safe-zone pass, the ending),
  send a CRF 20–22 preview under 30 MB, and list what was verified.
