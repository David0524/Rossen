# N. NAME — role

**Name:** Who they are in one short line, as a lower third would read. Deborah
Barbier, trial attorney, Columbia SC.

**Why them:** One sentence. Not two. She won the $45 million hidden-camera verdict.

**Prior on camera:** Outlet, date, URL. Or `None found`. No caveats about not
having watched it — the reader knows and this is internal.

**Contact:** A real email, phone number, or LinkedIn. Not a team page, not a
newsroom index, not "route through comms." If there isn't one, cut the guest.
  Source: where it came from, YYYY-MM-DD
  Pitch: one sentence on what they get out of appearing

**Bookability:** `STRONG` / `SOME` / `NONE FOUND` plus one clause of appearance
evidence. Nothing predictive.

**Confirmed:** What is verified and where. Facts only.
