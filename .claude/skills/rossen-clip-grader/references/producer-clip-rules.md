# Clip rules from the producer team (kit, Sept 2026)

Apply when grading or picking clips. These come from Matt Raub and Ryan Chaffee.

- **About two outside clips per segment** is Matt's standard. Outside clips must
  be cleared and shouldn't crowd Jeff off camera. Grade for the best two, not
  the most.
- **Local news is the best clip source** and the heat signal. A local news clip
  over 200K views is real heat; Ryan picks topics partly because "they usually
  have a lot of local news clips available."
- **Freshest clip first.** Note each clip's upload date and length. Confirm the
  date (`rossen-pre-bible/scripts/video_info.sh VIDEO_ID`); search results
  don't show it and "trending" clips are often a year old.
- **Links should be video.** If a clip lives on a news site rather than YouTube,
  flag it so Kyle pulls it before the taping. (Two clips on the Sep 25 F2 were
  news-site links and had to be flagged in Jeff's brief.)
- **Victim or bust footage beats explainers.** A victim telling their story with
  a dollar figure, dashcam or doorbell catching the act, police bodycam of the
  arrest. The Victorville seller's own dashcam and the father-son arrest carried
  the oil-in-the-engine story.
- **Body cam videos:** daytime footage, let raw footage breathe, fewer host
  interruptions, longer runtimes.
- **Don't name private individuals** from body cam or victim footage in titles or
  descriptions, and keep charges out of descriptions when someone is charged but
  not convicted.
- **Machine transcripts of body cam audio are rough.** Proofread names, dollar
  amounts and legal terms before an outcue or quote goes in a bible.
