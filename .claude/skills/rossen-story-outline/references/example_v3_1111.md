## HERE'S HOW TO PROTECT YOURSELF
`A8 · the fix`

1. **CHECK YOUR BUSHES… AND YOUR NEIGHBOR'S** (the 30-second walk from the tease)
   - Look in bushes, planters and trees, and along fences.
   - Look for anything pointed at your front door or driveway: a small camera or an old phone, a battery pack, tape, fake leaves or fake grass.
   - Check across the street too. Many of these point at the house across from them.
   - Trim the bushes that face your door, and ask whoever does your yard to keep an eye out.
2. **FOUND ONE? DON'T TOUCH IT. CALL POLICE.**
   - Don't move it. Don't pull it out.
   - Leave it there and call police. That's exactly how police set their trap.
3. **GET A CAMERA THAT KEEPS RECORDING**
   - A camera that needs Wi-Fi can go blind. So what holds up against a jammer? Listen.
   - **CLIP 7 (HORIZONTAL):** [NBC News — Thieves using Wi-Fi jammers to evade home security cameras](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LuNTbekmqnM) · Dec 6, 2025 · 1:34
     - **1:03–1:17 ("RECORDS ON AN SD CARD")**
     - *Soundbite: "The first thing is you can get a hardwired system that is not dependent on Wi-Fi. Plus, consider a locking parcel box, schedule deliveries for when you're home, and opt for a camera that records on an SD card."*
   - A system that doesn't need Wi-Fi at all is best. If not, get one that records to a memory card inside it. If the Wi-Fi gets cut, it keeps recording.
4. **GET TO KNOW YOUR NEIGHBORS**
   - In one neighborhood, several homes were hit within a few weeks. These neighbors didn't buy a new gadget. They went out and met each other. Listen.
   - **CLIP 8 (HORIZONTAL):** [ABC7 Los Angeles — 7 arrested in LA County home burglaries (North Hollywood)](https://abc7.com/videoClip/19140194/) · May 21, 2026 · 2:25
     - **1:16–1:51 ("WHAT'S GOING ON")**
     - *Soundbite: "After several homes being burglarized here in recent weeks, they've gone out to get to know their neighbors… 'We have massive text chains where we know first, last names… we'll keep each other updated on what's going on.'"*
   - Start a group text with the people on your street. Tell each other when you're traveling, and when something looks off.
5. **GOING AWAY? MAKE YOUR HOUSE LOOK LIKE SOMEONE'S HOME**
   - Don't post your trip until you're back.
   - Have a neighbor grab the mail, the newspaper and any packages.
   - Put your lights on timers.
   - Have the Post Office hold your mail. It's free, for up to 30 days.
