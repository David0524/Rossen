# Template: assignment email to Amanda

Sent after Jeff approves. Copy Jeff and Ryan.

```
Subject: [F1 or F2] assignment — [show frame] ([story A], [story B])

Amanda,

Jeff signed off on [which show]. [One line on the frame.] Same bible format as [a recent one].

A. Headline Jeff will understand.
- What happens, in order, in two or three bullets.
- The frame for Jeff: [the callback or the reason it's ours].
- Need: [the victim to find, the demo, the company response].
- Close: [the viewer advice].
- Source, date: link
- Source, date: link
- Source, date: link

B. Headline.
...

Fillers if we're short: [one or two quick hits with a link each].

Guest: [who to book and what they need to do on camera. If someone is already booked and may not fit, say so and give alternatives.]

Working titles: [two]

[Deals block and sponsor breaks as usual.] Bible by [day].

Matt
```

## Rules

- Tell her what to find, not just what the story is: a victim with a dollar figure, a demo, the company's response on the record.
- Put the freshest clip first. Note the date and length of each video.
- Include our own earlier episodes when there's a callback, with the timestamp.
- One guest ask per show, with a fallback.
