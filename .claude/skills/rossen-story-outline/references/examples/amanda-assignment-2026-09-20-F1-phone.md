# Example: assignment email to Amanda (Sep 20, 2026)

Subject: F1 assignment — "Your phone is the target" (new-phone intercept + number porting)

Amanda,

Jeff signed off on next F1. Two scams that come for your phone itself. Neither has been on the channel. Same bible format as Kroger.

**A. They knew your new phone was on the porch.**
- A woman's new iPhone is delivered. Minutes later "Xfinity" calls, knows the order and the model, says it's the wrong device, and emails a FedEx return label. The label ships the phone to the scammer. The carrier says they never called, and she's still paying for it.
- It's new-iPhone season, so these boxes are landing on porches all month. That's the hook.
- Beats: how they knew (breach or insider on the carrier or shipper side), the FedEx label trick, why the carrier won't eat the cost, and the close: no carrier calls you to send a phone back; if they do, hang up and call the number on your bill.
- Need: the Boston 25 victim or one from the help line, and the carrier's response on the record.
- Boston 25, Aug 24: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xd1S3AfqJHM
- Daily Press, Sept: https://www.dailypress.net/news/local-news/2026/09/cell-phone-delivery-scam/
- BBB Scam Tracker report: https://www.bbb.org/scamtracker/lookupscam/1164206

**B. They stole her phone number. Then her bank.**
- Port-out fraud. Scammers call your carrier pretending to be you, move your number to their phone, and every bank text and security code now goes to them.
- New tactic: they get you to read back a "verification code" that's actually your port-out PIN.
- A Palm Coast couple on Social Security had their landline ported and lost thousands. That's our viewer.
- Beats: the couple, how fast the takeover happens once the number moves, then the five-minute fix: set a port-out PIN and account lock with your carrier. We'll put the steps for Verizon, AT&T, and T-Mobile on screen.
- WFTV, Sept 15: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BpfOqSt5i3o
- FCC port-out fraud alert: https://www.fcc.gov/consumers/scam-alert/port-out-fraud-targets-your-private-accounts
- Verizon on unauthorized port-outs: https://www.verizon.com/about/account-security/unauthorized-port-outs

**Guest:** please book an expert for this one. Stickley is the obvious call, he can show the takeover live once a number moves. If he's not available, someone from the FCC or a carrier fraud team who can walk the PIN setup on camera.

**Working titles:** Your New iPhone Just Arrived — DON'T Answer This Call / They STOLE Her Phone Number — Then Every Bank Code Went to THEM

Deals block and sponsor breaks as usual. Bible by Wednesday.

Matt
