## C. Flood cars are about to hit the lots.
- Carfax: ~45,000 flood-damaged cars expected to enter the used market this hurricane season. Title washing across state lines. Carfax is not an NMVTIS provider; check VehicleHistory.gov. Physical tells: rust on seat springs, water line on seatbelts, damp padding, corrosion under the dash.
  - https://www.autosuccessonline.com/carfax-flood-damaged-cars-for-sale-hurricane-season-warning/
  - WRTV Investigates: https://www.wrtv.com/news/wrtv-investigates/warning-that-flooded-vehicles-could-soon-be-for-sale-to-unsuspecting-consumers

  - Heat signal for the lane: Wendover "How Car Dealerships Scam America," May 5, 1.15M.
