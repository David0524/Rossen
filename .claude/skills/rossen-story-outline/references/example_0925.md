# A. THE CALL AFTER YOUR NEW PHONE ARRIVES

**In one sentence:** Minutes after you activate a new phone, "your carrier" calls with a shipping-error story and a return label, and the phone goes to a scammer.

**Peg:** New iPhone season. Scammers time it to September promos.

**Weight:** ~20% of show · 2 clip beats

| # | Beat | Who | What happens | Clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The hook | You (the viewer) | New phone arrives, you activate it, and twenty minutes later caller ID says your carrier is calling. | JEFF |
| 2 | The call | Massachusetts woman, new Xfinity phone | She follows the "return" instructions and the phone is routed to a Maryland townhouse. | HAVE · HORIZONTAL — local news |
| 3 | How they know | The scammers | They have your name, address and phone model, and offer a gift card or threaten a penalty. | JEFF |
| 4 | The pressure | People posting online | Hours-long deadlines, a courier already booked, a prepaid label or QR code. | STILLS — 4 pop-ups |
| 5 | It gets worse | Utah man, never ordered a phone | A phone he never ordered shows up; he sends it back and gets billed for months. | HAVE · HORIZONTAL — local news |
| 6 | How it works | Jim Stickley, ethical hacker | Caller-ID spoofing, and the victim data comes from the dark web. | GUEST |
| 7 | Spot it | Jim Stickley | No carrier calls asking for a delivered phone back. Hang up and call the number on your bill. | GUEST |

### The fix

- A carrier will not call asking you to return a phone you already received.
- Hang up and dial the number printed on your most recent bill.

### Gaps

- ⚠️ Beat 2: Xfinity refunding her and disabling the phone is single-source. Confirm in the clip or a second source.

**In one sentence:** Scammers pose as you to your carrier, move your number to their phone, and use the texted codes to get into your bank and social accounts.

**Peg:** Rides A — same season, same criminals; FCC alert on port-out fraud.

### The fix

- AT&T: profile, Wireless Account Lock, on.
- Verizon: Profile and settings, Security, Number Lock, on.
- T-Mobile: T-Life, Account, Add products, Port Out Protection, on.
