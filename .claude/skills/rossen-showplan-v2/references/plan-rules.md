# Show Plan rules

What every section must contain. The template is the structure; these are the rules.

## Whole document

- **Bullets, not prose.** Easy to see, easy to edit. Short lines.
- **No producer names.** It's always Kyle or David; don't label owners.
- **No "decisions for Jeff" and no "why now" section.** Those are settled in PreBible V2.
- **One doc per show, created once.** Every later change is in place (see SKILL.md).
- No key or legend at the top. The doc opens with the title and Status line, then Run of show.

## Run of show

- Headlines only, in air order: Tease, stories, sponsor(s), good-news closer, End.
- Under each story, the clip IDs and a few words each: `Videos: [1a] Elijah's Target TikTok · [1b] …`
- Sponsors sit after Story 1 or Story 2 only — never mid-story, never after the closer.

## Tease

- Teases the **entire show**: every story, strongest first, the good-news closer framed
  as "then, good news," and a hold-back last (the reason to stay to the end).
- Each line is a fact or a reveal, never a topic. "A text that drains your account in
  30 seconds," not "a new scam."
- Every figure in the tease matches a ☑ claim in its story. No tease promises what
  the story doesn't deliver.
- Always very strong. If a line isn't alarming or surprising, rewrite it.

## Stories (threat stories)

- **Story 1 is the lead:** about 40% of the show and half the videos. Most people.
- **Hook:** one or two lines — the thing that makes someone stay.
- **How it works:** the mechanics in the order they happen.
- **People, least to most shocking.** Each person: who they are (a role, not a pronoun)
  — what happened — the twist. Under each:
  - `Turn:` the one line that makes it worse than the last.
  - `Video [ID]:` SOUND/B-ROLL · H/V · the searchable specifics ($, relationship,
    platform/company, agency or object) · the question the clip answers · link.
  - `[ID] IN/OUT: —` — filled by Clips V2. Keep the ID; it is the edit anchor.
  - When no person exists for a beat, label it `Beat N:` and say so in Open questions.
- **Stills & screen share:** screenshots, stills and walkthroughs are separate from
  video. A screen share lists every click, with exact labels.
- **Claims:** one per line — `☑ claim — source link` or `☐ claim — what's missing`.
  Include the fairness line (company denial/response) as its own claim.
- **Graphics:** full-screen numbers and lists, each tied to a ☑ claim.
- **Guest:** name, role, why them.
- **Protect yourself:** actionable steps. Settings paths list exact menu labels.
- **Open questions:** only what research couldn't settle — clearance, a device check,
  a choice the producers must make.

## Good-news closer (Wednesday Story 4)

- Never a scam. A money-saver, price cut, or what-not-to-buy.
- `The good news` · `What we're shown — and why it surprises` (demos with Video [ID]
  lines) · Stills & screen share · Claims · Graphics · `How to get it`.

## Sponsor segues

- One section per booked sponsor.
- `Out of:` the story it follows · `Bridge:` the thread from that story to the sponsor
  (thematic, not generic) · `Into:` the sponsor and the one point the read must hit.
- Describe the sponsor from its own materials; don't invent product claims.

## Friday

- One content story at full Wednesday quality, usually vertical clips and a screen share.
- Deals list: product — retail — deal — code/%. Nothing invented; every price checked.
- Live requests slot.

## A finished plan has no placeholders

Before handing to producers, search the doc for template leftovers (`[headline]`,
`[claim]`, `[sponsor]`, `URL`), IN/OUT lines still ending in `—`, "TBD," "not pulled," "find." Anything left is either filled or moved to Open questions
with what was tried.
