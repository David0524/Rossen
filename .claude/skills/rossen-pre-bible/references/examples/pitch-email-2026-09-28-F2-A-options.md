To answer the F1/F2 question first: Prime Day is the F1 main story for Friday. Fast food moves to a B or C. Target is pulled. Below are four new A-story options for F2, all scams, all checked against what's performing on other channels and what we've aired in the last three months.

**1. THEY STOLE HER HOUSE WITH A PIECE OF PAPER**

Deed fraud. Scammers forge a deed, record it at the county, and sell or borrow against a home the owner still lives in, and paid-off homes are the target because there's no lender to catch it. New Jersey's State Commission of Investigation just put out a 55-page report on it this month (it opens with the woman who tried to steal the title to Graceland), and Harris County sued nine people two weeks ago over a ring that took at least 10 homes. We touched it as a B story in April; nothing since.

Coverage:
- Epic Real Estate, Aug 28 (2.07M views): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rlxysfWUkmM
- Holy Schmidt, Aug 9 (857K views, "Retirees, once you pay off your mortgage..."): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5QPLImV9PgY
- NJ State Commission of Investigation report, Sept 2026: https://www.nj.gov/sci/documents/Final%20Deed%20Fraud%20Report%209-9-2026.pdf
- Hoodline, Sept 14 (Harris County sues nine, 10+ homes, families locked out): https://hoodline.com/2026/09/harris-county-sues-nine-accused-of-stealing-at-least-10-homes-via-fake-deeds/
- Yahoo Finance, Sept 10 (FBI warning to paid-off homeowners): https://finance.yahoo.com/real-estate/articles/fbi-issues-warning-us-homeowners-105500748.html

Potential Packaging:
- Your House Is PAID OFF? Scammers Can SELL It Without You — FBI Warning
- They Stole Her Home With ONE Piece of Paper — Check Your Deed NOW
- If You Own Your Home, Do THIS Today Before Someone Steals It

**2. THE SERIAL SQUATTER**

A Texas woman evicted nearly 30 times since 2005 just got 20 years for fraud. She forged her father's identity to get into a home, never paid, and worked the eviction process for months until the owner lost the house. It's a playbook: fake pay stubs, fake IDs, forged leases. Florida just made it a felony. This is the biggest competitor heat of anything I found, and we've never done it.

Coverage:
- CBS Texas, Sept 1 (1.64M views): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pTwLOlaBVVM
- ABC 10 News, Jul 23 (2.17M views, owner fights back after squatter takeover): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SwNN2Z2hPjQ
- Denver7 investigation (dozens of evictions, $40K restitution, owner lost her home): https://www.denver7.com/news/investigations/evicted-again-woman-evicted-dozens-of-times-in-colorado-and-texas-sentenced-to-20-years-in-fraud-case
- CBS12 (Florida's new rental-fraud law, HB 1293): https://cbs12.com/news/instagram/florida-law-fake-pay-stubs-and-rental-fraud-what-floridas-new-law-means-for-tenants-landlords-false-identity-counterfeit-documents-hb1293-rental-application-fake-id-bank-statement-impersonating-name

Potential Packaging:

**3. THE BITCOIN ATM AT YOUR GAS STATION**

The FBI says Americans lost $388 million through crypto kiosks last year, and 86% of it came from people 60 and over. Every impostor scam we cover ends at one of these machines. 35 states have now passed laws, 18 of them this year, and Minnesota's statewide ban took effect Aug 1. The footage is the sell: store clerks and cops physically stopping seniors mid-deposit. Competitor views are solid but older, so this is more news-driven than trend-driven.

Coverage:
- CNN (865K views, reporter gets walked to a Bitcoin ATM by a scammer): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UxegZ2eYsq4
- Good Morning America (police catch the scammer as the victim hands over money): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FB9o40ZSEpI
- AARP, updated Sept 14 (35 states, daily limits, bans): https://www.aarp.org/advocacy/crypto-atm-fraud-protections/
- FOX4 KC (clerk and police stop $30,000 scam on a senior): https://fox4kc.com/news/clerk-and-police-stop-30000-crypto-scam-targeting-liberty-senior/
- FBI IC3 alert, Sept 17 (names crypto kiosks as the payment method): https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2026/PSA260917

Potential Packaging:
- If Anyone Sends You to THIS Machine — It's a SCAM (Every Time)
- The Gas Station ATM That's DRAINING Seniors' Life Savings
- This Clerk UNPLUGGED the Machine to Save Her — The Bitcoin ATM Scam

**4. "YOU WON!" (YOU DIDN'T)**

Prize scams in three flavors: the stranger with a "winning lottery ticket" he can't cash (a 69-year-old in Queens lost $5,000 in jewelry in August), the Publishers Clearing House call, and the fake survey on the bottom of your store receipt. The advice is one line: real prizes never cost you money up front. Never on our channel.

Coverage:
- Law&Crime, Jun 26 (465K views): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uE_84BwH3no
- CBS Pittsburgh, Jun 5 (123K views, Lowe's receipt survey scam): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bn8_0tHSAgU
- QNS, Aug 10 (Woodhaven lottery ticket scam): https://qns.com/2026/08/woodhaven-crooks-lottery-ticket-scam/
- AARP sweepstakes scam guide: https://www.aarp.org/money/scams-fraud/sweepstakes/

Potential Packaging:
- "You Won!" — The Scam That Feels Like GOOD NEWS Until Your Account Is Empty
- DON'T Fill Out the Survey on Your Receipt — It's a TRAP
- A Stranger Has a WINNING Lottery Ticket for You — Here's What Happens Next

My pick is #1 for the A. It has the most views behind it, it's our exact audience, and the NJ report gives us a fresh peg. #2 has bigger raw numbers if you want something we've truly never touched.
