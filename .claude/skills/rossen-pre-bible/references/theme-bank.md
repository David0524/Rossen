# Theme bank

The themes to search from, Oct 25, 2026. Each has: what it threatens, how it has
done for us, what we've already aired (so a return needs new cases), and
**search directions** to explore. Search directions are *unresearched*: they are
places to look, never facts for a pitch. Lifts are from `performance-map.md`.
Ids match `scripts/theme_queries.json`.

Leads in Drive: the `Rossen Stories` doc (Kyle's scout) holds dated, sourced
leads that fit several themes below (caregiver takes the house, bank-merger
welcome letter, pastor impersonation, military funeral honors, phantom billing,
fake online stores, a stranger added to a card). Use them as **cases inside a
theme**, not as standalone pitches, and check the doc's "already with Jeff"
tab first.

## Winning lanes (search these every run)

### `phishing` — a message from someone you trust that isn't
- **Threatens:** your accounts, the moment you tap a link you had every reason to trust.
- **Record:** email and text scam shows ran 1.7× as a category; "Don't delete this email" (Sep) 467K; Gmail scam (May 13) 434K.
- **Already aired:** Gmail scam May 13; fake recall text Jun 12; Zelle fraud-alert text Sep 23; email bombing Sep 11.
- **Search directions:** patient-portal and hospital-bill messages (the MyChart case); bank-merger "welcome" letters and emails; toll and DMV texts (banked, never covered); fake data-breach notices; account-suspension emails from streaming or utility companies; pastor or church impostor texts.
- **Build it as:** "the trusted-sender scam," three senders, one tell (the link or the urgency), one move (go to the app or call the number on the card yourself).

### `mail` — the mailbox used against you
- **Threatens:** the checks, cards and letters you put in or take out of the box.
- **Record:** strongest category, 2.4×; envelope scam 1.1M; junk-mail letter 536K; delivery trap 715K.
- **Already aired:** envelope and brushing Jun 10; junk mail and address theft Aug 12; delivery scam Sep; Medicare card mailing Oct 9. **"It came in the mail" is dead as a frame** (used five times), so lead on what's taken, not on the mailbox.
- **Search directions:** check washing and mailbox fishing; stolen mail-carrier keys; change-of-address hijacks; fake breach notices by mail with QR codes; holiday package theft and fake missed-delivery slips (seasonal).

### `cards` — your card or account drained
- **Threatens:** the debit card, credit card, tap-to-pay and payment apps used every day.
- **Record:** 2.2×; debit card alert 2.8M (the channel's biggest); tap-to-pay 1.5M; Zelle 411K.
- **Already aired:** skimming May 20 and Sep 9; ghost tapping Sep 2; ATM cash trapping Sep 9; Zelle Apr and Sep 23; $20 distraction Jul 29, including a gift-card-draining segment (David, Oct 25: "we did this story already").
- **Search directions:** a stranger added as an authorized user; card-testing small charges; gift-card draining at the rack; bank conversions after mergers; fraud-department callback scams; holiday shopping card theft.

### `watched` — being watched without wanting it
- **Threatens:** privacy at home, in your car, on your phone.
- **Record:** 2.1×; apps spying 1.9M; TV spying 658K; smart device photos 463K. **Only when the viewer is the one watched and doesn't want it** (Flock flopped).
- **Already aired:** TV spying (Dec 2025, Jul 17, Oct 21); doorbell cameras; car spying; hidden cameras in rentals Aug 19; shopping-cart trackers Oct 7.
- **Search directions:** location sharing and AirTag-style stalking; voice assistants and recordings; hotel and rental cameras (seasonal travel); kids' devices and smart toys; data brokers selling your address.

### `home` — your home or property taken
- **Threatens:** the house, the deed, who gets in the door.
- **Record:** 2.2× (small sample); "You let them in" 307K.
- **Already aired:** home repair and contractors Mar 18; deed fraud as a B Apr 8; moving scams Jul 15; a signing-away-your-house story (caregiver power of attorney, equity theft) aired a few weeks before Oct 25 (David). Confirm the date and add it.
- **Search directions:** caregiver or relative takes power of attorney and the house; title theft (pitched Sep 28); squatters and forged leases (pitched Sep 28); door-to-door HVAC (help line); utility shutoff impostors (aired Apr 22, can return); storm-chaser contractors (seasonal).

### `online` — fake stores, fake sellers, scam ads
- **Threatens:** what you buy online and the card you buy it with.
- **Record:** 3.3× (5 shows); Facebook targeting 660K; YouTube scam ads 457K.
- **Already aired:** Facebook Marketplace Jun 17; scam ads on YouTube Jul 8; fake Amazon sellers and reviews; the "card declined" fake-store scam was filmed the week of Oct 19 (David). Confirm the air date.
- **Search directions:** fake holiday stores and fake tracking numbers; AI-made product ads; counterfeit safety products (car seats, smoke detectors); fake charity and GoFundMe pages around disasters.

### `impostor` — someone you'd obey or help, faked
- **Threatens:** your savings, through the authority or love you trust.
- **Record:** inside the phone and bank categories (1.7× to 2.2×).
- **Already aired:** FBI impostor Jun 3; jury duty Aug 26 and Sep 16; grandparent clip Sep 4; tech support Jun 24; Walmart call Aug 21; voice cloning May 13.
- **Search directions:** pastor and church impostors; military honors and funeral callers; fake police "your grandson is in jail" with couriers; bank fraud department callbacks; utility and IRS impostors at tax time.

### `arrest` — you could be the one blamed
- **Threatens:** your freedom and your name.
- **Record:** "arrest" titles 5.0×; self-checkout arrests 1.0M and 693K.
- **Already aired:** self-checkout Jan and Jul 1, Sep 29 video; license-plate and photo posting Sep 30.
- **Search directions:** unknowing money mules (romance or job victims charged); cloned plates and toll violations in your name; stolen identity used in a crime; package reshipping jobs.

## New territory (never built a show around)

- `cash_out`: Bitcoin ATMs, gold and cash couriers as the scammer's last step (Bitcoin ATM pitched Sep 28; never a segment).
- `prizes`: prize, lottery, sweepstakes and receipt-survey scams (Ryan: "very clickable"; never covered).
- `health_id`: medical identity theft and bills for care you never got (phantom billing). Health runs weak as a category, so frame it on the stolen identity, not the bill.

## Too niche (cut at H1)

- `family_death`: funeral, obituary and estate scams. David, Oct 25: "Too niche. Our audience is [only] relating to this one … if they had a recent death."

## Consumer-news themes (consumer track, added Oct 28, 2026)

No scammer; a company, store or rule does it to the viewer. Grade these on the
consumer track in `quality-gate.md`. Lifts are from `performance-map.md`.

### `company_watching` — the company is watching you
- **Threatens:** privacy through the phone, TV, car, store and smart devices.
- **Record:** 2.0× (17 shows). Apps spying 1.9M; TV 659K; smart device photos 464K.
- **Already aired:** TV (Dec 2025, Jul 17, Oct 21); stores spying (Jan); car spying (Jan); doorbell settings (Mar); shopping-cart tracking (Oct 7); Alexa recording (Jul 17).
- **Search directions:** robot vacuums and home maps; hearing aids and health apps; smart meters; retail facial recognition, if the viewer is the one flagged.

### `rules_ban` — a store or company rule that bans, arrests or charges you
- **Threatens:** being refused, banned, accused or charged at a place you shop.
- **Record:** ban and arrest titles 2.3× to 22×; self-checkout arrests 1.0M; Amazon returns ban 443K.
- **Already aired:** self-checkout (Jan, Jul 1, Sep 29 video); Amazon return bans (Nov 2025, Mar 20, Apr 24 Keep-It).
- **Search directions:** return tracking at walk-in stores (The Retail Equation reports); receipt checks at the door; account closures at banks and payment apps; holiday return-policy changes.

### `bills` — a bill everyone pays
- **Threatens:** the monthly money: electric, phone, insurance, subscriptions.
- **Record:** electric bill 489K (4.2×).
- **Search directions:** winter heating and budget billing; carrier promo-credit bills; subscription auto-renew at year end.

Not consumer-track material (off the list or weak): deals and price comparisons (Friday deals slot only); travel-only stories (0.1×–0.7×); lawsuits and payouts.

## Weak lanes (B, C or Friday only)

Retail and brands (0.9×), Amazon policy (0.9×), travel and dining (0.7×), deals
(0.5×). When one of these is needed, use its fear version: the scam at the store,
the arrest at the register, the thief at the airport. Never refunds or payouts.
