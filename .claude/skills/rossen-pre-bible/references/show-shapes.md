# Show shapes and slot fit

Load when checking a slate against the day, or deciding how many stories to
research. Current as of late September 2026, from the producer kit
(`producer-kit/show-formats-2026-09.md`). This replaces the older "Wednesday is
four stories, always four, with a good-news closer" shape.

## F2 — taped Friday, posted Wednesday

Two forms. **Ask which one before researching.**

**The story show.** A, B, usually C, plus an expert (car scams, taped Sep 25:
three stories with Lauren Fix).

| Slot | What it needs |
|---|---|
| **A** | A scam with a villain, broad enough for the whole audience, big enough to carry the show and the packaging (Jeff: "the bulk of the show"). A human victim with a dollar figure, clips, the extended threat, and a one-line viewer advice. Not niche, not Target, not a deals story. |
| **B** | Same shape, shorter. Can be a consumer story without a villain ("fast food bringing back humans: B or C only"). Often a live screen demo. |
| **C** | A quick hit or a third short story. |
| **Expert** | One guest ask with a fallback. Regulars: Jim Stickley (demos), Amy Nofziger (AARP), Lauren Fix (cars), Jason Zirkle (ACFE). |

**The call-in show.** Two viewer callers, audio only, plus an expert. The
strongest caller is the A and is packaged like any other video. Sourcing is the
help line (`producer-kit/help-line-summary.md`, caller IDs only). Full format:
`producer-kit/call-in-show-format.md`. Book five or six to land two or three.

## F1 — Friday, live

A lead scam or consumer story (sometimes an A and a B), usually a guest expert,
a sponsor read, then the Amazon deals block with DealSeek (Trey Donovan or Derek
Couture) and live chat requests.

- **Deals stories belong here.** The Prime Day price check "works for the Friday
  show."
- The lead can survive on a screen-share walkthrough instead of clip beats.
  Decide that at pitch stage and say so under `FOOTAGE — BE HONEST ABOUT THIS`.
- Never invent deals, products, or prices. The deals block is not this
  document's job.

## Other lanes (not ours to pitch)

Retail videos (Monday, Thursday), the monthly Kirkland series, standalone body
cam videos (Tuesday). A monthly deal-focused Friday show tied to shopping events
runs through January.

## The SLOT FIT line

Every story page carries one line testing that story against its proposed slot:

```
**SLOT FIT:** F2 A — carries it. A scam with a villain, victim with a dollar
figure, two strong local news clips, Stickley can demo the fix.
```

```
**SLOT FIT:** F2 B — fits. Not a scam, so it can't be the A (Jeff's rule).
```

```
**SLOT FIT:** ⚠️ Proposed as F2 A, but it's a deals story. Jeff sends those to
the F1.
```

```
**SLOT FIT:** ⚠️ F2 A, but the Aug 5 show covered this and flopped. Dead
unless there's a genuinely new angle.
```

Be blunt. A story that doesn't fit its slot is more useful flagged than quietly
reslotted.

## The slate verdict

Page one, under the shortlist:

> **SLATE FIT:** Supports the F2: four A options (recommend #1), a B and a C.
> The F1 lead is already set (Prime Day), so nothing here is pitched for it.

> **SLATE FIT:** ⚠️ Only two A candidates survive the coverage check. Jeff
> wants four or five. Candidates to research are under THE GAP.

When the slate is short, add a `THE GAP — AND WHAT I'D RESEARCH NEXT` section at
the back: unresearched concepts, ranked by distance from what already aired,
each labelled clearly as **not researched**. Good places to look first: the
story board's "Banked for later" table and the help-line clusters.
