## 1. Gold bar scam: COVERED, twice

- **Jul 15, 2026 livestream "You Let Them In — Then They Steal EVERYTHING You Own"** (xC5US2KqvhM). Full segment roughly 21:41 to 27:05, teased at 01:07. Jeff calls it "the new gold rush scam." Bank/government impostor, spoofed caller ID, convert life savings to gold, courier picks up. Victim who lost $300K+ and sold her house. Florida clip with the coin-shop owner. Close is verbatim the pitch's close: "Never, ever, ever convert money to gold. If anyone unsolicited tells you to do it, don't do it." Ends with a courier bust caught on camera (Antonio Pena and Brandy Durst).
- **Jun 3, 2026 livestream "NEW SCAM ALERT: If You Answer, THEY STEAL EVERYTHING!"** (wmMzpVYF9Xw). Lead segment 00:19 to 15:19 on FBI impostor scams. $850K couple. Decoy-box sting with the real FBI after a daughter intervened ($86K drop). Courier arrested with $85K in gold bars from a Seattle victim, second courier Navjot Singh arrested in a gold-bar sting.
- Also Jun 24 "The New PHONE TRAP" leads with the pop-up / tech support on-ramp (00:38 to 14:29), which is the "Stickley on-ramp beat" in the pitch.

What's new in the pitch: NYPD $100M number, NY AG Aug 7 warning, the Sept 2 Hicksville sting with the dollar-bill serial number code word, the 18-year sentence, the Maryland takedown. The frame, the close, and "police sting with decoy gold" are not new.
