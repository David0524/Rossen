AGED-CARE LIVING, SELLING POSSESSIONS SCAM
-SCAMMERS ARE POSTING PHOTOS OF CARS, LAWN MOWERS, BOATS, AND OTHER BIG TICKET ITEMS.
-THE PRICE LOOKS AMAZING… THEN COMES THE STORY:
"MY GRANDFATHER IS MOVING INTO A NURSING HOME."
"MY DAD IS GOING INTO ASSISTED LIVING."
"WE NEED TO SELL EVERYTHING QUICKLY."
"JUST TRYING TO HELP THE FAMILY."
-IT SOUNDS LIKE AN OPPORTUNITY TO HELP SOMEONE.
-BUT INVESTIGATORS SAY MANY OF THESE LISTINGS ARE COMPLETELY FAKE.
-THERE IS NO GRANDPA… THERE IS NO NURSING HOME.
-THERE IS NO BOAT… NO CAR… NO LAWNMOWER
-JUST A SCAMMER TRYING TO COLLECT DEPOSITS.
-THEY WILL OFTEN SAY THERE IS HIGH INTEREST AND THAT THEY NEED A DEPOSIT.
-THEN THEY WILL HOLD THE ITEM FOR YOU… THEN THEY DISAPPEAR.

FAKE CAR ACCIDENT DEATH SCAM
-THIS MAY BE THE CRUELEST FACEBOOK SCAM WE'VE SEEN YET.
-SCAMMERS ARE POSTING MESSAGES CLAIMING SOMEONE YOU KNOW HAS DIED IN A CAR ACCIDENT.
-THE POST OFTEN INCLUDES A PHOTO… A NAME… AND A HEARTBREAKING STORY.
-THEY WANT YOU TO FEEL SHOCKED… TO PANIC.
-AND MOST IMPORTANTLY… THEY WANT YOU TO CLICK.
-THE POST USUALLY SAYS SOMETHING LIKE:
"I CAN'T BELIEVE HE'S GONE."
"REST IN PEACE."
"LOOK WHO DIED IN THIS ACCIDENT."
-THEN IT HAS YOU AND A BUNCH OF YOUR FRIENDS TAGGED… AND IT INCLUDES A LINK.
-CLICK IT AND YOU MAY BE TAKEN TO A FAKE FACEBOOK LOGIN PAGE DESIGNED TO STEAL YOUR PASSWORD.
-IN OTHER CASES IT MAY INSTALL MALWARE OR COLLECT PERSONAL INFORMATION.
-ONCE CRIMINALS GET INTO YOUR ACCOUNT, THEY CAN USE YOUR PROFILE TO TARGET YOUR FRIENDS AND FAMILY NEXT.
-THAT'S WHY THIS SCAM SPREADS SO QUICKLY.
-IT PREYS ON HUMAN EMOTION… AND IT WORKS.

WHAT IS AN I-M-E-I NUMBER?
I-M-E-I STANDS FOR INTERNATIONAL MOBILE EQUIPMENT IDENTITY.
-THINK OF IT AS A PHONE'S FINGERPRINT OR SERIAL NUMBER.
-EVERY LEGITIMATE CELL PHONE HAS A UNIQUE 15-DIGIT I-M-E-I NUMBER THAT IDENTIFIES THAT SPECIFIC DEVICE.
-WIRELESS CARRIERS USE THE IMEI TO DETERMINE:
WHETHER A PHONE HAS BEEN REPORTED STOLEN
WHETHER IT'S BLACKLISTED
WHETHER IT'S STILL UNDER FINANCING
WHETHER IT'S ELIGIBLE TO BE ACTIVATED ON A NETWORK
WHETHER IT HAS BEEN INVOLVED IN FRAUD
