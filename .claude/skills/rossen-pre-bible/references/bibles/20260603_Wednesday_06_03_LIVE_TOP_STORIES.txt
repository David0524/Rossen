HERE’S HOW TO FIGHT BACK:
-ALWAYS CHECK THE UNIT PRICE
-THAT SMALL NUMBER ON THE SHELF TELLS YOU THE REAL COST PER OUNCE OR PER SHEET.
-COMPARE PACKAGE WEIGHTS
-DON’T ASSUME THE “FAMILY SIZE” IS ACTUALLY A BETTER DEAL.
-WATCH FOR WORDING CHANGES
-“NEW LOOK” OR “IMPROVED PACKAGING” CAN SOMETIMES SIGNAL LESS PRODUCT INSIDE.
-BE CAREFUL WITH BULK BUYING
-SOMETIMES THE BIGGER PACKAGE IS ACTUALLY MORE EXPENSIVE PER OUNCE.
-USE STORE BRANDS
-IN MANY CASES THEY HAVE NOT SHRUNK AS AGGRESSIVELY AS NAME BRANDS.
-TAKE PHOTOS OF PRODUCTS YOU BUY REGULARLYIT HELPS YOU NOTICE WHEN THE SIZE CHANGES.
