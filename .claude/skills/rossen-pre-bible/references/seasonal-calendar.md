# Seasonal hooks

Rung 2 of the peg ladder in `story-search.md`: when there's no fresh news peg,
tie the theme to the time of year **of the airdate**. These are recurring
seasons and dates, not news. Confirm any specific date for the current year
before it goes in a pitch (deadlines and sale dates move).

| Month | What's happening for our viewers | Themes it pegs |
|---|---|---|
| January | New year, winter bills, W-2s arrive, tax season opens | `impostor` (IRS and tax), `phishing` (tax-document emails), `home` (heating and utility shutoff), `cards` (holiday-shopping fraud shows up on statements) |
| February | Valentine's Day, tax filing in full swing | romance scams and money mules (`arrest`), `impostor` (IRS), `phishing` (refund-status texts) |
| March | Tax season peak, spring cleaning, travel planning | `impostor`, `phishing`, `online` (fake travel and rental listings) |
| April | Tax deadline mid-April, spring storms, home projects | `impostor` (tax), `home` (contractors, storm chasers) |
| May | Graduations, moving season starts, summer travel booking | `home` (moving), `online` (rental and booking fakes), `family_death` is evergreen |
| June | Summer travel, heat, Father's Day | `watched` (hotel and rental cameras), `cards` (travel card theft), `home` (AC repair) |
| July | Prime Day, peak travel, heat | `online` (fake deals and stores), `phishing` (fake order emails), `watched` |
| August | Back to school, hurricane season building | `online`, `phishing` (school and account emails), `home` (storm contractors) |
| September | Hurricane season peak, fall travel | `home` (storm chasers, flood cars), `online` (fake disaster charities) |
| October | Medicare open enrollment opens mid-October, Prime fall sale, Cybersecurity Awareness Month, Halloween | `impostor` (Medicare and benefits callers), `phishing`, `online`, `watched` |
| November | Black Friday and Cyber Monday, holiday shipping, Giving Tuesday, Medicare enrollment closes early December | `mail` (package theft, fake missed-delivery slips), `online` (fake stores, fake tracking), `phishing` (order and delivery texts), fake charities |
| December | Holiday deliveries, gift cards, holiday travel, year-end giving | `mail`, `cards` (gift-card draining), `impostor` (grandparent and family emergency calls around holiday travel), fake charities |

Year-round: anything tied to a death in the family, a hospital stay, a move, or
a new phone, car or bank. Those life moments are a peg any week.
