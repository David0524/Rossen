# What performs: the live-show performance map

Snapshot of every Rossen Reports live stream (148 shows, oldest through Oct 21,
2026), built Oct 25, 2026 for the "Live Show Story Map" dashboard. Views are
YouTube's rounded public counts read that day.

**Lift** = a show's views ÷ the median views of all live shows from the same
month. 1.0× is a typical show; 2.0× is twice typical. It corrects for the
channel's growth and for older videos having had longer to collect views.
Shows under two weeks old are left out. Use these numbers to decide **where to
spend search effort**, not as facts for air.

Caveats: categories were assigned from titles, transcripts and the aired log;
small categories (under six shows) swing on one hit; the oldest month bucket
lumps everything from Oct 2025 back, so its lifts run high. Ask for a refreshed
map when the YouTube Analytics export (retention, click-through) arrives.

## Fear framing

Titles built on a threat (steal, drain, spy, arrest, targeted, trap, danger,
don't, never, stop) ran a median **1.7×**; the other titles ran **0.8×**
(81 vs 63 shows). "Drain" titles ran 2.6×, "spy" 7.8× (4 shows). "Deal" ran
0.5×, "secret" 0.3×, "hidden" 0.5×, "Amazon" 0.7×.
