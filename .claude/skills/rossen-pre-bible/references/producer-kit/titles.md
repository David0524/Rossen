# Title style guide

Taken from the channel's own uploads. Always give three options.

## The patterns that repeat

| Pattern | Real examples |
|---|---|
| The NEW [X] Scam — THIS [thing] DRAINS Your Account | The NEW Wrong Number Scam — THIS Reply DRAINS Your Account! |
| If You See THIS [thing] — [command or consequence] | If You See THIS Amazon Sticker — DON'T TOUCH What's Inside! |
| [ALERT LABEL] — [what it does] | CREDIT CARD ALERT — This NEW "Tap to Pay" Scam Drains EVERYTHING On Your Card |
| This GENIUS [X] Scam Steals EVERYTHING — Do THIS Now! | This GENIUS Envelope Scam Steals EVERYTHING — Do THIS Now! |
| DON'T [do the normal thing] | The NEW Delivery Scam EXPLODING in 2026 — Don't Return THIS Package! |
| Your Bank WON'T / CAN'T | Don't Say NO to THIS Zelle Text — Your Bank WON'T Refund You |
| They [did X] — What [happened next] | They Stole THIS From Walgreens — What Police Did Next Is WILD! |

## Rules

- Two halves joined by an em dash. First half names the threat. Second half is the command or the stakes.
- Two to four words in CAPS: THIS, NEW, DON'T, EVERYTHING, DRAINS, STEAL, a number.
- "THIS" hides the reveal. Use it.
- Words that recur: NEW, EXPLODING, DRAINS, STEALS EVERYTHING, TRAP, TARGETED, GENIUS, DEVASTATING.
- Put the viewer in it: you, your.
- A dollar figure or a number when there's a strong one.
- No proper names of victims or suspects.
- Titles change after upload. The grocery show went up as "The NEW Supermarket TRAP" and was later retitled "The NEW Food Ripoff." Offer alternates.
