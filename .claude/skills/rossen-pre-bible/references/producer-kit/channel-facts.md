# Channel facts: what the data says

Source: Matt's back-catalog teardown, built August 27, 2026 from YouTube Studio exports. 501 videos, March 2025 to August 2026. Medians, not averages. Videos under about 60 days old were left out as still maturing. Revenue figures are left out of this kit on purpose; ask Matt.

Numbers drift. Date-stamp anything you quote, and check Studio before it goes in a public post.

## The channel

- 99.3M lifetime views and about 645K subscribers as of August 27, 2026. Older documents say "620K+" and "600K+." Use the current Studio number.
- Long-form median views per video roughly quadrupled in 2026: about 58K in January to about 190 to 242K in July and August. Median click-through rate rose from 4.9% to 5.7%.
- Livestream median, March to September 2026: about 180K (from the upload list in this kit).

## Who watches

- 62% of views come from viewers 55 and over. Viewers 65 and over are 37% of views and 52% of watch time.
- Gender: the teardown export showed about 50/50, with women watching longer (7:05 vs. 5:17 average). An older note says about 75% female. These conflict. Check Studio before citing either.
- Returning viewers are 70% of views and 82% of watch time. It's a habit audience.
- New viewers click less (4.26% vs. 6.05% for returning viewers). Packaging is the lever for new viewers.

## What performs

- **Scams and fraud is the franchise engine.** Best subscriber conversion at scale, highest revenue per view, top revenue topic. Median 118K views across 41 matured videos.
- **Costco and Kirkland are reliable reach** (245K median) but convert subscribers at about half the rate of scam content.
- **Dated recurring series work.** "[Store] Is Discontinuing THESE Items — Prices Just CRASHED (Month Year)" and "NEW at [Store]: 10 Items That WON'T LAST" run 400 to 740K.
- **Live shows convert subscribers at about twice the rate** of regular uploads.
- **Laggards:** food and recall topics, airline and travel topics.
- **Retail performers:** Dollar Tree, Costco, and Walmart "discontinued" lead. Aldi lags because its products rotate less often. Home Depot climbed from low to mid-pack through steady iteration.
- **The audience cares about deals, not trends.** A Walmart viral-style video bombed.

## Proven scam winners and one instructive flop

- Winners: junk mail, delivery drivers, self-checkout, debit card skimming, robocalls.
- Flop: Flock license-plate cameras. It was trending, but our viewers likely support neighborhood cameras. **A trending topic dies if our viewer isn't the one being targeted.**

## Runtime

| Length | What happens |
|---|---|
| 8 to 12 min | Dead zone. Modest views, low revenue per view. |
| 12 to 16 min | Most views (262K median). |
| 25 to 60 min | Earns the most per view and converts subscribers best. Half the catalog is already here. |

## Days and cadence

| Day | Median views |
|---|---|
| Wednesday | 242K. Best day, with a 5.7% median click-through rate. |
| Monday to Thursday | All fine. |
| Friday | About 88K. |
| Saturday | About 27K. |
| Sunday | Nothing published. |

Four to five long uploads a week is the working ceiling. Weeks with six showed lower per-video medians.

## Traffic

- Browse 34%, Shorts feed 43% (historical), Suggested 11.6% with the longest watch time, Search 2.9%.
- External traffic is 1.4% of views, despite about 1.1M followers on other platforms.

## Shorts

Stopped in June 2026 because they were too time-consuming for the return. They drove about half of lifetime views and about 18% of subscribers gained in the trailing year, but almost no revenue. Matt treats the decision as settled. If anyone proposes restarting, they need both halves of that fact.
