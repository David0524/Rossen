# The shows, as of late September 2026

From the producer kit. Where this disagrees with older wording in
`show-shapes.md` or `rossen-script-writer`, this is current.

| Day | What posts |
|---|---|
| Monday | Retail: "What's New At" and "10 Items That WON'T LAST" (not ours to pitch) |
| Tuesday | Dark, or a standalone body cam / investigation video (10 to 13 min) |
| **Wednesday** | **The F2.** Best day by far (242K median, 5.7% CTR). |
| Thursday | Retail: "Last Chance" and "Discontinuing" |
| **Friday** | **The F1, live, with the deals block.** Also the shoot day for both shows. |

- **F1 (Friday, live).** A lead scam or consumer story, usually a guest expert,
  a sponsor read, then the Amazon deals block with DealSeek and live chat
  requests. Sep 25 ran an A and a B plus the expert. Deals guests have been Trey
  Donovan and Derek Couture; confirm who's on and make every cue match.
- **F2 (taped Friday, posts the following Wednesday).** Example: taped Oct 2,
  posts Oct 7. No deals block. Jeff defined it in Sept 2026 as the viewer
  **call-in show** (audio-only callers, two stories plus an expert: see
  `call-in-show-format.md`). Since then some F2s have been standard **A, B, C
  story shows with an expert** (car scams, taped Sep 25, with Lauren Fix). Ask
  which one this F2 is.
- **So "Wednesday" in a request usually means the F2**, and the pitch deadline
  is the Friday taping, not the Wednesday post. Time-sensitivity is still scored
  against the post date.
- **A monthly deal-focused Friday show** tied to shopping events is planned
  through January (Black Friday run targets Nov 13 and Nov 27).
