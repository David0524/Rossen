## The read

The scam channels prove how big the content can get, but they serve younger, broader audiences. The 55 and over viewer, who is the actual scam target, has no channel built for them. That's our lane. Ryan's caution: don't set expectations off their subscriber counts. Measure against our own trajectory.

## The Pleasant Green question

Jeff asks regularly why his scam videos don't match Pleasant Green's numbers. The prepared answer:

- Pleasant Green posts about two handcrafted documentaries a month, roughly 4.6M monthly views. That's his whole operation.
- Our long-form alone did 5.9M in May, 3.9M in June, and 5.3M in July 2026, on less than half his subscribers, while publishing daily.
- Comparing per-video numbers is apples to oranges. The real lesson from him is one consistent format.

## How this applies to competitor searches

Ryan's rubric looks for topics overperforming on other channels. When you run the competitor search:

- News stations and consumer channels are the signal. A local news clip over 200K or a creator explainer over 500K is real heat for us.
- Scam-baiting and entertainment channels (Scammer Payback, Kitboga, penguinz0) get huge numbers on content we don't make. Their views don't tell you a topic works for our viewer.
- Real estate and retirement creators (Epic Real Estate, Holy Schmidt, Bob Sharpe) share our audience. Their hits are strong signals.
- Body cam channels validate the body cam lane, not a scam topic.
