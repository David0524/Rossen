# Rossen Reports: standing rules

- **Every video opens on a 1–2 s intro title card.** It titles the video and carries the official ROSSEN REPORTS logo (`intro/assets/official_logo.png`, drawn from its file, untouched, after the print finish), plus whatever short line helps (e.g. the show day and time). Keep it on the beat grid: a pickup of whole beats before bar 0 (the Wednesday tease uses 3 beats = 1.875 s at 96 BPM). The card is on screen, level and still, from frame 0; it leaves before the transition into the first scene. Reference: `sceneIntro` in `intro/wed-tease.js`.
- **Jeff's logo sits in the corner throughout every video** (Jeff's request): `logoBug()` in `intro/vertkit.js` draws the official logo, 140 px wide, top-left corner at (40, 48), untouched and opaque, in screen space after everything else. Not on the intro title card or the outro (the closing card, or a tease's loop). Top-left, tight in the corner (the client found it too low at y 300): clear of the platforms' buttons (right side) and captions (bottom) and of our caption chips; the app's own top-left label can briefly overlap it in the feed. The LIVE TODAY loops carry their own logo and get no bug.
- **Series (JEFF'S RULES, WHAT WOULD YOU DO?)**: their recurring opens already act as the title card, and their recurring ending (including the rules FOLLOW FOR stamp) is approved as it is.
- **Every live-show tease ends on its LIVE TODAY loop played twice.** Append the approved loop untouched (stream copy, never re-rendered) two times after the tease; each join must be the loop's own seamless wrap. (Reference build: `intro/tools/build_wed_tease.sh`; the older Friday Amazon tease's `build_tease.sh` is not a template.)
- **Before planning a new film, read `STUDIO_NOTES.md`** (the critique loop, motion and acting rules, banned defaults) and, for motion and rigs, `.claude/skills/claude-animation/references/`. Score the contact sheet and fix the three worst problems before any full render.
- **Every Rossen animation: use the `rossen-animation` skill** (`.claude/skills/rossen-animation/`, V2). It holds the client's standing direction (`references/craft.md`, formerly `intro/SKILL_NOTES.md`), the film types and their reference builds, the tease chapter, the client's review notes, and the brief templates to hand the user (`templates/brief.md`, `templates/brief-tease.md`).
