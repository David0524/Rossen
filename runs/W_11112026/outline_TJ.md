## HERE'S HOW TO PROTECT YOURSELF

1.  **CHECK YOUR BUSHES... AND YOUR NEIGHBOR'S**

    -   Look in bushes, planters and trees, and along fences.

    -   Look for anything pointed at your front door or driveway: a
        > small camera or an old phone, a battery pack, tape, fake
        > leaves or fake grass.

    -   Check across the street too. Most of these point at the house
        > across from them.

    -   Trim the bushes that face your door, and ask whoever does your
        > yard to keep an eye out.

2.  **FOUND ONE? DON'T TOUCH IT. CALL POLICE.**

    -   Don't move it. Don't pull it out.

    -   Leave it there and call police. That's exactly how police in
        > Massachusetts set their trap.

3.  **GET A CAMERA THAT KEEPS RECORDING**

    -   So what holds up against a jammer??? Listen.

    -   **CLIP 8 (HORIZONTAL):** [[NBC News --- Thieves using Wi-Fi
        > jammers to evade home security
        > cameras]{.underline}](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LuNTbekmqnM)
        > · Dec 6, 2025 · 1:34

        -   **1:03--1:17 (RECORDS ON AN SD CARD)**

        -   *SOUNDBITE: "The first thing is you can get a hardwired
            > system that is not dependent on Wi-Fi." "Plus, consider a
            > locking parcel box, schedule deliveries for when you're
            > home, and opt for a camera that records on an SD card."*

    -   Wired is best if you can afford it. If not, get a Wi-Fi camera
        > that records to a memory card inside the camera. If the Wi-Fi
        > gets cut, it keeps recording, and you can pull the footage
        > later.

    -   Don't count on the cloud alone. If they block the connection,
        > nothing makes it to the cloud.

4.  **GET TO KNOW YOUR NEIGHBORS**

            > Now we have a massive group. We have massive text chains

    -   Start one with the people on your street. Tell each other when
        > you're traveling, and when something looks off.

    -   They're the best security camera you'll ever own.

5.  **GOING AWAY? MAKE YOUR HOUSE LOOK LIKE SOMEONE'S HOME**

    -   Don't post your trip until you're back.

    -   Have a neighbor grab the mail, the newspaper and any packages.
        > Gone a few weeks? Get someone to keep the lawn cut.

    -   Put your lights on timers.

    -   Have the Post Office hold your mail. It's free, for up to 30
        > days.

    -   Even a security sign or window sticker can send a burglar to the
        > next house.
