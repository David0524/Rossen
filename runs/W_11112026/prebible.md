### STORY 1 — BURGLARS WATCHING FROM THE BUSHES

**Confirmed:**
- Braintree, MA: break-in Jun 20, 2024 while the family was away; neighbor found a camera taped in the bushes across the street; police had the family fake a vacation; on Jul 11, 2024 a man returned to the camera and was arrested after a foot chase, two women in a getaway car. Lt. Det. Brian Cohoon: "We set a trap, they took the bait." Police tie the three to "South American Theft Groups" (CBS Boston, WCVB, Jul 16, 2024).
- West Hills, LA, Mar 20, 2025: neighbor saw two people put something in a bush at ~2 a.m.; at ~11 a.m. four or five masked people left a home with bags and went back to the camera. No arrests reported (CBS LA, Mar 20, 2025; KTLA via Yahoo, Mar 21, 2025).
- Rancho Cucamonga PD, Oct 6–8, 2026: cameras near homes on two occasions in two weeks; no arrests; "don't touch or remove them" (CBS LA, Oct 7, 2026; KTLA via Yahoo, Oct 8, 2026).
- LA County, May 20, 2026: DA Hochman, Sheriff Luna, LAPD Chief McDonnell, Ventura Co. Hard-wired systems are harder to disable (ABC7, NBC LA, Westside Current, May 20–21, 2026).
