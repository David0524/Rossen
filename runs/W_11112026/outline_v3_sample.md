## SUMMARY

Police say burglary crews hide cameras in bushes across the street to learn when a family leaves, and one Massachusetts neighbor's find let police stage a fake vacation and catch the crew. It's still happening, in Southern California this fall, and the same crews move fast, jam Wi-Fi cameras and knock dressed as delivery drivers. Then Jeff walks a yard and shows exactly what to look for, what to do if you find one, and how to lock down everything else they're watching.

## HERE'S HOW TO PROTECT YOURSELF

*A8–A9 · The two-minute walk; lock down the rest · Jeff Rossen, in a front yard (Nov 2026); North Hollywood neighbors (May 2026)*

1. **TAKE THE TWO-MINUTE WALK** ← the promise from the tease
   - Check bushes, planters, trees and fences, and the yard across the street, for anything pointed at your door or driveway (Whittier PD via [KTLA](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/hidden-camera-found-aimed-burglarized-221017663.html); Brea PD via [KTLA](https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/police-warn-o-c-residents-063805599.html); LASD via [Patch](https://patch.com/california/los-angeles/hidden-cameras-found-outside-homes-likely-planted-burglary-stakeouts-lasd)).
   - What it looks like: a small camera or old phone, a battery pack, tape, fake leaves or fake grass.
   - Trim the shrubs that face your door, and ask whoever does your yard to look too (Whittier PD; Brea PD; LASD Lost Hills via [ABC7](https://abc7.com/post/burglary-tourists-hidden-cameras-break-socal-homes-authorities/14885445/)).

   **DEMO (show-produced):** Jeff walks a front yard to a mock bush camera: where it hides, what gives it away.
   - **Jeff should know:** don't show anyone kicking or pulling a camera out (a West Covina homeowner did); police say leave it.

2. **FOUND ONE? DON'T TOUCH IT. CALL POLICE.**
   - Don't move it or pull it out. Leave it and call police. That's how Braintree set the trap (LASD; Rancho Cucamonga PD; Brea PD).

3. **GET A CAMERA THAT KEEPS RECORDING**
   - A system that doesn't need Wi-Fi is best. Otherwise, a camera that records to its own memory card keeps recording if the Wi-Fi is cut (NBC News experts; Sheriff Luna via [ABC7](https://abc7.com/post/7-arrested-socal-home-burglaries-tied-south-american-theft-group-authorities-say/19138631/)).

4. **GET TO KNOW YOUR NEIGHBORS**
   - After a string of break-ins, these neighbors started a group text.

   **CLIP 7 (HORIZONTAL) · PICK** — [ABC7 — 7 arrested in LA County home burglaries (North Hollywood)](https://abc7.com/videoClip/19140194/) · May 21, 2026 · 2:25

   **1:04–1:15 ("LIKE WI-FI JAMMERS") // BUTT 1:16–1:51 ("WHAT'S GOING ON")**

   *Soundbite: "Recommendations to secure your home include… cameras that are hardwired, that are less vulnerable to burglar instruments like Wi-Fi jammers." // "Residents in this North Hollywood neighborhood told us that after several homes being burglarized here in recent weeks, they've gone out to get to know their neighbors. 'Now we have a massive group. We have massive text chains where we know first, last names… we'll keep each other updated on what's going on.'"*

   - **Out:** Your neighbors are the best camera you'll ever own.

5. **GOING AWAY? MAKE THE HOUSE LOOK LIVED IN**
   - Don't post the trip until you're home; tell a neighbor or police when you'll be away (LA County officials via [ABC7](https://abc7.com/post/7-arrested-socal-home-burglaries-tied-south-american-theft-group-authorities-say/19138631/)).
   - Lights on timers (LASD via Patch). Have the Post Office hold your mail: free, 3 to 30 days ([USPS](https://faq.usps.com/articles/FAQ/USPS-Hold-Mail-The-Basics/1000)).
   - Do-nothing test: the walk alone still leaves the jammer and the knock, so steps 3–5 close those. Do nothing, and a crew can watch for one to three weeks (Kent County, MI sheriff's sergeant via [WOOD TV](https://www.woodtv.com/news/kent-county/deputies-organized-theft-ring-targeted-home-in-cascade-township/amp/)).
   - **Jeff should know:** crews also put GPS trackers on cars to know when owners are at work (Kent County deputies; Abington, PA police). One line if time allows.
