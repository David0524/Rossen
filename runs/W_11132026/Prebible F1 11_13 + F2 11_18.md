# 2. THE PHONE NUMBER YOU GOOGLED BELONGS TO A SCAMMER

**Status:** READY · **Score:** 13/14 (Fear 2, Heat 2, Track record 2, Breadth 2, Peg 2, Newness 1, Producibility 2) · **Lead test:** passes 2 of 3

### POTENTIAL PACKAGING

- NEVER Google THIS Phone Number — Scammers Are Answering
- Google Is Showing SCAM Numbers — Check Before You Call Your Airline
- Before You Call Customer Service — Do THIS First

**Built on:** "Stop doing THIS on your phone" (2.7×) and the "NEVER do THIS" command; phone scams 1.7×.

### THE PITCH

You search for your airline, bank or Amazon's number, call the top result, and a friendly "agent" takes your card number or asks to get into your phone. On Oct 27, The Points Guy reported Google's AI answers showing scam numbers for American, United, Delta, Royal Caribbean and Expedia, planted on hacked websites. Thanksgiving travel is two weeks out. Frame it on the phone number, not on travel (travel runs 0.7×).

### CASES

- **Denver (2025).** Dan Smoker was charged $17,328 after an airline agent transferred him to a fake Lufthansa number the agent had found by searching online. [People via Yahoo, Aug 27, 2025.](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/traveler-says-fell-scam-cost-083000608.html)
- **River Forest, IL (July 2026).** A woman lost about $4,000 to fake "flexible" tickets; Amex refunded it after the ABC7 I-Team stepped in. [ABC7 Chicago, Jul 23, 2026.](https://abc7chicago.com/post/river-forest-woman-recovers-4k-fake-icelandair-support-scam/19560002/) CHECK: her number came from an email link, not a search.
- **Boston (Oct 2026).** A traveler called a number Google showed for Swiss International Air Lines; a $4,500 charge followed, and Amex blocked it. [The Points Guy, Oct 27, 2026.](https://thepointsguy.com/news/travel-scam-google-ai-search-results/) No loss.

- Thieves Are FOLLOWING Seniors Home From the Bank — Do THIS Before You Leave

# 4. YOUR BANK WILL NEVER SEND THIS PERSON TO YOUR DOOR

**Status:** NEEDS A CLIP · **Score:** 11/14 (Fear 2, Heat 1, Track record 2, Breadth 2, Peg 2, Newness 1, Producibility 1) · **Lead test:** passes 1 of 3

### POTENTIAL PACKAGING

- Your Bank Will NEVER Send THIS Person to Your Door
- DEBIT CARD ALERT — Scammers Are Coming to Your HOUSE for It
- If "Your Bank" Says a Courier Is Coming — DON'T Open the Door

**Built on:** "DEBIT CARD ALERT" (2.8M, 12×), the channel's biggest live show.
