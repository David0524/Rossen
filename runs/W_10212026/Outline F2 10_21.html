The fix

  • After She Prepares (beat 3): if your TV asked you to "agree" to anything at setup, assume the tracking is on.

  • After Texas (beat 4): you're not in Texas, so nobody is going to ask you. Check it yourself.

  • After Gamers Nexus (beat 5): don't wait for the listening claim to be settled; turn off what's proven.

  • Look in privacy or viewing settings first; the names below are where it usually lives, and they vary by model. ⚠ verify on 2026 sets

  • Samsung: Viewing Information Services, off (Consumer Reports, how to turn off smart TV snooping, updated 10/19/2025).

  • LG: Viewing Information (Live Plus), off (Consumer Reports).

  • Vizio: Viewing Data, off (Consumer Reports). Vizio says turning it off won't affect how the TV works (Vizio privacy policy, per She Prepares, 9:14).

  • Roku TVs: Use Info from TV Inputs, off (Consumer Reports).

  • Sony: Samba Interactive TV, off (Consumer Reports).

  • No setting on your TV? ⚠ Lon to confirm the fallback (e.g. take the TV off Wi-Fi and use a streaming device); unsourced.

  • Microphone: Roku, Microphone Access to Never Allow; Samsung's physical mic switch. ⚠ unsourced, Lon to confirm.

  • Do-nothing test: do nothing and the tracking stays on. Opting out did stop data going to the tracking servers in one study's tests (cited by She Prepares, 6:27). ⚠ Check whether a software update or factory reset turns it back on.

  • The one setting the tease promises is ACR, under each brand's name above (Consumer Reports).

The fix

  • After the woman in York County: a letter from an energy company you don't know isn't junk. Open it.

  • Your utility won't call or knock to "lock" your rate. Close the door or hang up, and call the number on your bill. ⚠ source (your utility's scam page)

  • Find the supplier and "price to compare" lines on your bill. ⚠ source (state utility commission page; the term varies by state)

  • Switched without your OK? File a complaint with your state utility commission (Pennsylvania PUC, per WPXI and FOX43). ⚠ confirm on the PUC page

  • Never give your account number to a caller or a door-to-door agent (Illinois Attorney General, Rushmore Energy settlement, 6/4/2026). ⚠ confirm the AG's page says it

  • Budget billing to spread winter costs (your utility). ⚠ source

  • LIHEAP for help paying heating bills, through your state. ⚠ verify 2026–27 funding (acf.hhs.gov)

  • Do-nothing test: if you were already switched, doing nothing keeps you on their rate. Call your utility to switch back, and ask about cancellation fees. ⚠ source
