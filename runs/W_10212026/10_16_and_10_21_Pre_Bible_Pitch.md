### PACKAGING

- If Someone Calls About BAIL Money — Do THIS Before You Pay a Dime

*Thumbnail: a county jail roster with one name circled, a phone ringing.*

### WORKING TEASE

> THE CALL COMES THE SAME NIGHT. "YOUR NIECE WAS ARRESTED. SHE NEEDS BOND."
> YOU CHECK THE JAIL WEBSITE. SHE'S REALLY THERE.
> SO YOU PAY. AT A BITCOIN ATM.
> SHE NEVER GOT OUT. THE BONDSMAN WAS FAKE.

### THE HOOK

Sheriffs from Louisiana to Minnesota to Georgia warn that scammers read public booking lists and call the families of people just arrested, posing as bondsmen or deputies. In Pinellas County a family lost about $2,000 at a Bitcoin ATM the same evening their niece was booked (WFLA, Aug 6). A sheriff in Minnesota: "When the family checks the jail roster, it shows that the individual is indeed newly in jail."

### THE PAYOFF

- **Hang up and call the jail yourself** on the number from the sheriff's website.
- Bail is paid at the jail, the clerk or a licensed bondsman, never by gift card, crypto, Zelle or a Bitcoin ATM.
