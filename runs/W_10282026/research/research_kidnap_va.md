## (A) Fake / "virtual" kidnapping calls

### Confirmed facts
- **FBI IC3 PSA I-120525-PSA, Dec 5, 2025**: https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2025/PSA251205 (loaded). "Criminals Using Altered Proof-of-Life Media to Extort Victims in Virtual Kidnapping for Ransom Scams."
  - Contact usually starts by **text**. Scammers alter photos "found on social media or other publicly available sites."
  - Tells: "missing tattoos or scars and inaccurate body proportions."
  - Scammers use **timed (disappearing) messages** so victims can't study the image.
  - Advice: family code word, try to reach the loved one before paying, "Screenshot or record proof of life photos whenever possible," be careful posting missing-person information, report at ic3.gov.
- **KIRO 7 / MyNorthwest, May 21, 2026** (James Lynch): https://www.kiro7.com/news/local/fbi-warns-ai-driven-virtual-kidnappings-that-use-voice-cloning-fake-photos-target-families/TJACYYSDHJAOVCGVQNBU6BASF4/
  - An FBI warning about AI-driven virtual kidnappings that combine voice cloning with altered or fabricated photos and video.
  - Adds odd backgrounds and lighting as tells. Advises limiting posts about real-time location and travel plans.
  - The article names **no FBI field office, no spokesperson and no cases**.
- **Lynchburg (VA) Police, WDBJ7, Jan 12, 2026**: https://www.wdbj7.com/2026/01/13/lynchburg-police-warn-virtual-kidnapping-scam-targeting-families/
  - Local reports, but no named victims or dollar amounts.
  - Kathleen Jennings (LPD): "They prey upon urgency and fear..." Scammers tell victims not to contact anyone, including police.
- **Knox County Sheriff (state not stated; the station is in Monmouth, IL), 97.7 WMOI, Sept 16, 2026**: https://977wmoi.com/2026/09/scam-alert-false-kidnapping-calls-targeting-families/
  - Callers claim a cartel, play a distressed voice and send the target to Walmart for a **prepaid card**, changing the location repeatedly.
  - No losses; the family members were found safe.
- **Action News Jax, Feb 2, 2026**: https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/fake-photos-real-fear-ai-224000816.html
  - FBI SA Matt Horton demonstrated mock AI "proof" images: "It doesn't have to be perfect."
  - **No real local case** with a photo or video.

## (B) VA benefits rerouted

### Confirmed facts
- **VA News, "Deepfake phishing: Don't be fooled," Oct 14, 2026** (author "carysummers," Office of Information and Technology, Cyber Month 2026): https://news.va.gov/149747/deepfake-phishing-dont-be-fooled/ (full text pulled via curl).
  - Verbatim: "a fake 'Veterans & Retired Seniors Grant Program' post circulated on Facebook that appeared to be sponsored by a government official. The post fooled some Veterans into handing over their direct‑deposit information, leading their VA benefits to be redirected straight into a scammer's pockets. So far, a number of Veterans have been targeted, losing thousands of dollars in promised VA payments. VA is working quickly to make each affected Veteran whole."
  - It calls this "deepfake phishing" and says AI makes "fake voices, videos or pictures (like the flyer on Facebook)" seem real.
  - **No exact count of veterans, total dollars, or named official.**
  - Advice: "Stop. Check. Then act." Call a trusted number or log in through a site you type in yourself. Report to the **VSAFE Fraud Hotline 833-38V-SAFE (8-7233)** or reportfraud.ftc.gov.
- **VBA Veterans Benefits Newsletter, Oct 1, 2026**: https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USVAVBA/bulletins/42c780e
  - Lists "payment redirection" among the threats.
  - Links a video by **VA Deputy CFO Jeanine Gilson** on direct-deposit payment redirection fraud.
  - If you see suspicious direct-deposit activity, "contact the VA right away at 1-800-827-1000."
  - Separate item: veterans and their families reported $584M in job-scam losses (FTC/BBB).
- **VA News, "Fraud Alert: Watch out for phishing scams," Sept 25, 2026**: https://news.va.gov/149788/fraud-alert-watch-out-phishing-scams/
  - Lists "Vishing (Voice Call): AI-generated deep-fake phone calls" impersonating government agencies.
  - General advice: multi-factor authentication; never share passwords.
- **VSAFE page**: https://www.va.gov/vsafe/ confirms "1-833-38V-SAFE (8-7233)" and www.vsafe.gov. No hours are listed.
