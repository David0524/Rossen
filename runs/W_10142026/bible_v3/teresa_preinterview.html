Teresa Murray: Pre-Interview

Rossen Reports · 10/14 F2 Wednesday · A story: mall kiosk scams

Jeff's four questions

  1. How do these mall kiosks pull you in? The sample… the compliment… the chair? Want: the step-by-step playbook, from the free sample to the back room.
  2. Someone steps out with a sample. What do you actually say to walk away? Want: an exact phrase viewers can use. Jeff follows with "take the sample and keep walking."
  3. Say it already happened. You got charged, and the sign says no refunds. How do you get your money back? Want: call the card company the same day, dispute it, then report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov (graphic on screen).
  4. These victims were too embarrassed to tell anyone. How do you talk to a loved one about this? Want: how to raise it without shaming them. Our audience is 55+, so it's about a spouse, a friend or a sibling, not "your parents."

Where she lands

Right after the three A-story cases:

  • Kathryn, 84 (Florida): went in for a $25 facial and was charged $26,000. The store refunded about $4,500.
  • Donna, 75 (Washington): got free eye cream at a kiosk, and the owner drove her to her bank for $50K. Two tellers called police.
  • Kentucky: a $100 screen repair at a phone kiosk killed the phone. The waiver said "not liable."
