# A. THIS FREE SAMPLE COST HER $26,000

**In one sentence:** Mall skincare stores hand older shoppers a free sample, sit them down, and run up bills in the tens of thousands, and one store just closed after CBS LA's reports.

**Peg:** The Bee & Co. store at Westfield Topanga closed Sept 24 after months of CBS California Investigates reports (latest Oct 1).

**Weight:** ~50% of show · 6 clip beats

| # | Beat | Who | What happens | Clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The hook | You (the viewer) | A worker at the mall hands you a free sample and asks you to sit down for a quick demo. | JEFF |
| 2 | The $25 facial | Kathryn Taylor, 84, Florida widow on Social Security | She goes in for a $25 facial and is charged about $26,000; the store refunds about $4,500 and nothing more. | HAVE · HORIZONTAL — First Coast News |

### The fix

- Never sit down for a "free" demo. Take the sample and keep walking.
- Ask for the total in writing before you hand over a card. Never sign a "final sale" form you can't read.
- Already charged? Call your card company the same day and dispute it; report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

### The fix

- Never make a paid caregiver your power of attorney. Name a family member or a lawyer.
- Sign up for your county's free property-fraud alert.
- Suspect it? Call Adult Protective Services through the Eldercare Locator, 1-800-677-1116.

# C. IF YOU SEE THIS VIDEO ABOUT YOUR PROPERTY TAXES, IT'S FAKE

**In one sentence:** A fake AI video tells seniors Congress froze their property taxes and pushes a form that doesn't exist, and the phone number reaches scammers who want your Social Security number.

**Peg:** Official warnings from Wisconsin (Sept 3–11), Ohio, California and Colorado, still circulating into October.

**Weight:** ~15% of show · 2 clip beats

| # | Beat | Who | What happens | Clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The fake video | Unnamed — scammers behind an AI video, no victim found | The video claims a new federal senior tax freeze and a "Senior Homeowner Tax Review Request" form. | FIND · HORIZONTAL BROLL — the scam video or a news grab of it |
| 2 | The warning | David Casey, Wisconsin revenue secretary, and county auditors | Officials say no such law or form exists, and the number in the video goes to scammers. | FIND · HORIZONTAL — local news warning |
| 3 | The fix | You (the viewer) | Property taxes are local; call the number on your tax bill and never give your SSN to "file." | JEFF |

### The fix

- Property taxes are set by your county and state, never by Congress. There is no federal form.
- Call your assessor at the number on your tax bill, not one from a video.
- Never give your Social Security number to "file" for a freeze.

### Gaps

- ⚠️ No victim found. The story is the warning, not a person.
- ⚠️ The "file by Oct 15" deadline is unsourced.
- ⚠️ Local warning clips are all under 1,000 views. The scam video itself may be down.
