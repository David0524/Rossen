## A · THE MALL SCAMS STEALING THOUSANDS OF DOLLARS FROM PEOPLE LIKE YOU

Shape: the skincare scam, the bust, then the mall phone repair kiosk.

### A1 · THE SCAM: SHE SAT DOWN FOR A $25 FACIAL. THEY CHARGED HER $26,000!!

**Setup:** Kathryn, 84, Florida. She went in for a $25 facial. They moved her around the store and never told her the total.

**Video:** [First Coast News: Fernandina Beach widow says $25 facial became costly mistake that cost her $26,000](https://www.firstcoastnews.com/video/news/investigations/ask-anthony/aa-pkg/77-f6f872e5-820a-4289-9558-af592db82a79). **Turn:** SO DOES ANYBODY EVER GET CAUGHT DOING THIS??? WATCH WHAT HAPPENS WHEN ONE OF THEM GOES TOO FAR!!

### A2 · THE BUST: THIS OWNER TOOK A 75-YEAR-OLD TO HER BANK… AND THE TELLER CALLED THE COPS!!

**Setup:** Donna, 75, Washington. Free eye cream at a mall kiosk, then a $5,000 spa program. Then the owner said, "Come on, we're going."

**Video:** [KOIN 6: Vancouver bank teller saves woman from $50K skin care "trance"](https://www.yahoo.com/news/vancouver-bank-teller-saves-woman-010000147.html). The bust. 0:32 to 0:48, butt 2:22 to 3:11. The first teller says no. He drives her to a second branch. That teller calls police.

**Turn:** AND THAT'S NOT THE ONLY SCAM AT THE MALL RIGHT NOW!!

### A3 · THE NEXT MALL SCAM: YOU HAND YOUR PHONE TO THE MALL KIOSK FOR A QUICK FIX… AND GET BACK A DEAD PHONE!!

**Setup:** Cracked screen. The kiosk in the middle of the mall says it can fix it while you shop. You hand over your phone and sign the form.

**Video:** not locked. The scraper is looking for a recent one. The only TV package found so far is from 2019: [WCPO: Woman learns downside of cell phone repair kiosks](https://www.wcpo.com/money/consumer/dont-waste-your-money/woman-learns-downside-of-cell-phone-repair-kiosks).

The 2019 case, if nothing newer turns up: Danielle Scruggs paid $100 for a new iPhone 8 screen at a Florence Mall kiosk in Kentucky. Half an hour later the phone kept shutting off and would not charge. The kiosk refunded the $100 and refused to pay for an $800 replacement. The form she signed said the company was "not liable for damages" during the repair.

**Payoff:** THE FORM YOU SIGN SAYS IF THEY BREAK IT, IT'S YOUR PROBLEM!!

**Needs from the scraper:** a victim on camera from the last 18 months, naming the mall, with the price quoted and the price charged.

**Backups if the kiosk footage does not come through:** wallet lifted in the mall and run at the Apple Store ([NBC6 Miami](https://www.nbcmiami.com/news/local/pair-stole-womans-wallet-at-aventura-mall-went-on-apple-and-target-spending-spree-cops-say/3827157/)), hug-and-switch necklace swap ([WJLA](https://wjla.com/news/local/con-artist-wanted-baltimore-woman-stealing-jewelry-off-victims-fairfax-county-crime-jewelry-theft-distraction-scam-pickpocketing-robbery-suspect-surveillance-video-police)), diamond swap at a mall jeweler (only if in a mall and on video).

### A · HERE'S HOW TO PROTECT YOURSELF

- DON'T SIT DOWN. Take the sample and keep walking. No chair, no back room.
- No card until you see the total, in writing.
- "All sales final" or "no refunds" on the counter? Leave.
- Nobody from a store goes to your bank with you. Tell the teller.
- At a repair kiosk: read the form before you sign it, and get the owner's name and number before you hand over the phone.
- Already charged? Dispute it with your card company today. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

**Video:** [CBS Miami: Home healthcare worker accused of stealing condo from elderly woman, disabled son](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/miami/news/home-healthcare-worker-accused-of-stealing-condo-from-elderly-woman-disabled-son). ### B3 · A CAREGIVER PUT AN 84-YEAR-OLD MAN'S NAME ON A $616,000 MORTGAGE… POLICE SAY HE HAD NO IDEA!!

**Who:** An 84-year-old man.

**What:** Found hurt on the floor of his home by his caregiver. Police say she had become his power of attorney and health care surrogate and cut him off from family and friends.

**Money:** One account went from about $106,000 to under $5,000. A $616,000 mortgage on her house lists him as co-borrower; police say he never knew.

**Still:** the Port St. Lucie police photo, in [CBS12: Caregiver drained elderly man's assets, used him on mortgage](https://cbs12.com/news/crime/florida-crime-news-port-st-lucie-police-caregiver-drained-elderly-mans-assets-used-him-on-mortgage-vulnerable-grand-theft-65-older-neglect-elderly-disabled-exploitation-estate-documents). No video.

**Status:** The 60-year-old caregiver was arrested with help from U.S. Marshals. Grand theft, exploitation, neglect. Accused, not convicted. He is back with his family.

### B · HERE'S HOW TO PROTECT YOURSELF

- Power of attorney: never sign it over to a paid caregiver. Name a family member or a lawyer.
- Your loved one signs nothing the caregiver hands them, timesheets included. Family reads it first.
- Look up the home on your county property appraiser or clerk website. Then sign up for a property-fraud alert.
- Call a lot, sometimes without warning. AARP Maryland: tell the caregiver before hiring that family calls weekly, plus surprise calls.
- Look at their bank statements yourself, every month.
- Something feels wrong? Call Adult Protective Services. Eldercare Locator, 1-800-677-1116. On screen.

## C · SELF-CHECKOUT IS OVERCHARGING YOU RIGHT NOW… AND SHOPPERS ARE CATCHING IT ON CAMERA

**Hook:** BE HONEST… DO YOU ACTUALLY WATCH THE PRICE ON THE SCREEN? ONE SHOPPER DID, AND WHAT SHE CAUGHT IS GOING VIRAL!! One can of lasagna, then the settlements, then the new digital tags.

### C1 · THE WALMART SIGN SAID $1… IT RANG UP 44% HIGHER!!

**Who:** Amanda, a shopper in Kansas.

**What:** Filmed a rollback sign over the Chef Boyardee lasagna, grabbed one can, hit record at self-checkout. An employee overrode it to $1, but only after she caught it.

**Money:** Sign said $1. Rang up $1.44. THAT'S 44% MORE THAN THE SIGN SAID!! And 7% more than the old price of $1.34, so the "sale" cost more than no sale at all.

**Video:** [TikTok @amandabainum457](https://www.tiktok.com/@amandabainum457/video/7686390447178026253). Vertical. No timecodes in the script. Out: YOU SAW WHAT IT RUNG UP AT. She shows the $1 rollback sign, scans one can, and the screen reads $1.44.

**Bridge out:** THAT'S ONE CAN. HOW MANY THINGS IN YOUR CART DID YOU ACTUALLY CHECK???

### C2 · AND IT'S NOT ONE STORE… WALMART AND DOLLAR GENERAL HAVE PAID MILLIONS OVER THIS!!

**Dollar General:** $1.55 million to settle with Pennsylvania, announced Dec. 9, 2025. The attorney general says its stores failed more than 40 percent of price checks. THAT'S 4 OUT OF EVERY 10!!

**On screen:** the Pennsylvania Attorney General's own headline. [Screenshot this release](https://www.attorneygeneral.gov/?p=32538).

**Walmart:** $5.6 million to settle a California case, August 2025, over registers charging more than the lowest posted price. Walmart didn't admit wrongdoing.

**On screen:** KTLA's headline, which sits over a photo of shoppers at a Walmart self-checkout. **Video:** None. These two are full-screen stills.

### C3 · NOW WALMART'S PRICE TAGS ARE TURNING INTO SCREENS… AND ONE MAN SAYS THE MATH DOESN'T ADD UP!!

**Who:** Jimmy Wrigg, a consumer advocate in Tennessee.

**What:** Walmart is switching shelves to digital price tags, part of the California settlement's plan to get prices right. Wrigg checked the small per-ounce number under the big price. He says a viewer flagged a salt tag the same way.

**Money:** On a 2.25-ounce jar of McCormick Italian seasoning, he says the per-ounce price works out to about $5.09. The big price on the same tag is $2.24 more.

**Video:** [TikTok @james_wrigg](https://www.tiktok.com/@james_wrigg/video/7690598362671779086). Vertical. No timecodes or out cue in the script. He stands at a digital shelf tag and does the per-ounce math on camera.

**Response:** Walmart, still a placeholder in the script.

### C · HERE'S HOW TO PROTECT YOURSELF

Graphic 001 runs over these.

- The 5-second habit: watch the screen as each item scans.
- Snap a photo of the sale sign before you leave the aisle.
- Rings up higher? Stop and ask for the shelf price before you pay. Kansas law says a store can't charge more than the posted price.
- Check your receipt before you walk out the door, not at home.
- Keeps happening at the same store? Report it to your state attorney general.
