# NEW STORY — DON'T DIAL THIS CODE: IT SENDS YOUR BANK CALLS TO SCAMMERS

*Airdate TBD (11/9 new-story batch) · one story · no guest · 1 break · Stage: videos*

## SUMMARY

Someone calls pretending to be your bank, says there's a problem, and asks you to dial a short code to "secure your account." That code turns on call forwarding, so every call meant for you, including the bank's own verification calls, rings the scammer instead. A Pennsylvania couple lost $63,000 this way, and the fix is simple: never dial a code a caller gives you, hang up and call the number on the back of your card, and know the code that turns forwarding off.

## WATCH WHAT HAPPENS WHEN I DIAL IT
`A4 · the demo`

- This isn't a hack. It's a normal phone feature called call forwarding, and scammers are using it against you.
- On many phones, star 72 plus a number sends all your calls to that number. On others, the code is star 21.
- I'm going to dial it on my phone, and send my calls to my producer.

**DEMO (show-produced):** Jeff dials *72 and a producer's number on a test phone. A second producer calls Jeff's number; Jeff's phone stays silent and the producer's phone rings. Then Jeff dials *73, the same call rings Jeff's phone again.

- **Out:** That's it. Now, when your bank calls to check a big transfer, the scammer picks up and says "yes, that was me." Here's how to make sure that never happens to you.

## HERE'S HOW TO PROTECT YOURSELF
`A5 · the fix`

1. **NEVER DIAL A CODE A CALLER GIVES YOU** (the one rule from the tease)
   - Not star 72, not star 21, not anything. Your bank will never ask you to.
2. **HANG UP AND CALL THE NUMBER ON THE BACK OF YOUR CARD**
   - Even if caller ID says it's your bank, and even if they know your information.
3. **CHECK THAT YOUR CALLS AREN'T BEING FORWARDED**
   - On your phone, go into Settings, then Phone, and look for Call Forwarding. It should be off.
   - Or dial the off code: star 73 on Verizon; pound pound 21 pound on AT&T and T-Mobile.
4. **IF YOU ALREADY DIALED IT, ACT TODAY**
   - Turn forwarding off, then call your bank and your phone company from a number you trust.
   - Change your banking passwords, watch your accounts, and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. There it is on the screen.

An RIT cybersecurity lecturer, Jonathan Weissman, said the scammer likely used the forwarding to catch the bank's verification call.
- A2: the station says star 72 forwards calls, not texts. Say "calls."
- A3: March 2025. The member didn't go on camera and isn't named. No money was lost; the credit union stopped the $27,000 transfer. Say "asked for $27,000," not "stole."
- A3: in the clip the credit union says star 72 redirects "calls and text messages." Other sources say texts aren't forwarded. Keep Jeff's own copy to calls.
- A4: codes differ by carrier. Verizon uses *72 to turn forwarding on and *73 to turn it off; AT&T and T-Mobile use *21* or **21* with the number, and ##21# to turn it off. Use a test phone and a producer's number, never a real viewer's.

## The fix: sources

- Step 1: never dial a code a caller gives you ([KeyBank: Be aware of the *72 scam](https://www.key.com/about/security/be-aware-of-the-72-scam.html); [Vision Bank, Feb 27, 2026](https://www.visionbank.bank/resources/blog/call-forwarding-scam); Patty Smith, Commonwealth Credit Union, in [WKYT](https://www.wkyt.com/2025/03/31/credit-union-warns-call-forwarding-scam)).
- Step 2: hang up and call the number you know ([WKYT](https://www.wkyt.com/2025/03/31/credit-union-warns-call-forwarding-scam): "call them back at the number they have listed on their website"; [KeyBank](https://www.key.com/about/security/be-aware-of-the-72-scam.html): "a number you know to be correct"; [abc27 via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/trust-bank-thieves-stole-lemoyne-231209547.html)).
- Step 3: check forwarding and the off codes ([Verizon: call forwarding, *72 on, *73 off](https://www.verizon.com/articles/verizon-unlimited-plans/call-forwarding-everything-you-need-to-know/); ##21# for AT&T and T-Mobile per carrier guides and [Openbank's warning via Euro Weekly News, Oct 2026](https://euroweeklynews.com/2026/10/22/bank-in-spain-warns-customers-not-to-dial-this-phone-code/)). The AT&T and T-Mobile off code comes from third-party guides; confirm on each carrier's own support page before taping.
- Step 4: turn it off, call the bank, change passwords, watch accounts ([Vision Bank](https://www.visionbank.bank/resources/blog/call-forwarding-scam); [KeyBank](https://www.key.com/about/security/be-aware-of-the-72-scam.html); [Queen Anne's County, MD sheriff via WGMD, Sep 23, 2026](https://www.wgmd.com/warning-issued-about-phone-scam-using-call-forwarding/)); report it ([ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov)).
- Do-nothing test: forwarding stays on until someone turns it off, so a viewer who dialed the code weeks ago is still exposed. Hence step 3 for everyone, not just people who think they were scammed.
