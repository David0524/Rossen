### 1. THE "GIFT" THAT TAKES YOUR GOLD — San Jose Costco (IN, the open)

Two women, religious small talk, a "gift" necklace around an 86-year-old's neck while he loads groceries with his 75-year-old wife. His heirloom medallion (from the priest who married them) is gone, a fake left behind, and a second car with men blocks them in. **Adds:** a Costco, our viewer, a crew, and a fear beat in one case. **Tell:** a stranger who wants to put something on you. Clip: none confirmed on NBC Bay Area's page (check by hand); Salinas Costco backup, [KION](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sOIpLk3aszg).

### 2. THEY GOT HIM BACK HIS BROTHER'S RING — Renton, WA (IN: the result)

The "blessing" crew took a 71-year-old's necklace holding his brother's Tuskegee Airman ring. Bellevue detectives recovered it; three arrested and a fourth charged (not-guilty pleas). Surveillance and bodycam exist. **Adds:** Jeff's "show a real result early," and proof it's organized (Sacramento's sheriff says the same at Costco, Sam's Club, Walmart, Lowe's and Home Depot, targets mostly 50 to 70-plus, July 2026). Clips: [FOX 13 arrests](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X_pAsqSPpgY), [FOX 13 ring returned](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cRVoOUcA6_0). Taste: police name a nationality; we say "an organized group."

### 3. YOUR PURSE IS GONE, AND YOUR CARDS ARE DRAINED IN MINUTES (IN)

Vallejo Costco: a car camera catches a woman knocked down from behind while loading and her purse taken (Aug 2025, two minors arrested). Portland Costco: a 93-year-old's wallet lifted; $1,500 in gift cards before he got home (May 2024). Clarksville Sam's Club: an 82-year-old's purse snatched as she returned her cart (Jan 2026). Boynton Beach: police say two women in wigs and medical masks stole from older shoppers for months, then bought gift cards (Mar 2026). **Adds:** the caught-on-camera moment and the extended threat (your cards, within minutes). **Tell:** "excuse me, which way to...?" while your purse sits in the cart.

### 4. "REAL GOLD" FOR GAS MONEY — Mettawa, IL Costco, $15,800 (IN, framed as fraud)

A man in the Costco lot needed money "to get to the airport"; the next day he sold the shopper "$16,000" of jewelry for $15,800. Fake. Police staked out the lot, the SUV hit an undercover car, two men pleaded guilty and got probation. Same script in Kauai (Walmart, Feb 2025), Eugene (Mar 2026), Hillsboro (Lowe's, Aug 2025). **Adds:** the biggest dollar loss and a sting. **Tell:** a hard-luck story plus "real gold," cash only. Clip: [KREM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HXvfyXqtIoU) (pawn shop real-or-fake test), [KOAA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bF4ysj53h7U). **DEAD-LIST CAUTION:** keep it on the loss and the stakeout; no confrontation compilations, and many bodycam uploads carry a slur in the title. Don't use those.

### 5. YOUR CAR WON'T START, AND THE "HELPERS" ARE RIGHT THERE — Lehi, UT Costco (IN)

Her Corolla dies after shopping; two men offer a jump, pull a fuse, ask $200, then pour water on the engine when she says no. She says police saw them on Costco's lot footage circling and going under the car. TikTok 447K+ (per Motor1). Flat-tire version: Culpeper, VA Sheriff (Aug 2025), "a good Samaritan" points out the flat while a partner takes the purse; Vinnie Penn's retelling, 1.1M+ per Motor1 (a Starbucks, not a store lot). **Adds:** a new move, and the show's biggest reported clip numbers. **Tell:** the trouble starts while you're inside, and the help is already waiting.

### 6. A TRACKER ON YOUR CAR — Auburn, AL (IN: the stop-and-check payoff)

Her iPhone said a tracker was moving with her; the first ping was at Sam's Club, and she believes it went on at a Walmart. A magnetic GPS unit under the car (WTVM, Sept 2025). Also Fort Wayne (zip-tied under the bumper, Aug 2025) and Sulphur, LA (Nov 2025). **Adds:** the "check it right now" moment: Stickley hides one, both phones alert. Motive unknown: say "someone," and keep it off home burglary (11/11). An older Rossen Reports piece covered AirTags on cars ([KCRA](https://www.kcra.com/article/rossen-reports-thieves-tracking-apple-airtags/65665970)); the demo makes it new.

### 7. QUICK HITS (JEFF LINES)

Fake parking tickets on windshields at a mall with no meters (Hurst, TX PD, Apr 2025); its QR code is a callback to May 27. **Myth buster:** the "paper on your back window" carjacking warning is a hoax that has circulated since about 2004.

### STORY 1 — THE COSTCO PARKING LOT SCAMS

**Confirmed:**

- San Jose, Costco at Almaden Plaza: two women approached an elderly couple loading groceries, used religious talk, and put a "gift" necklace on the husband; his 18-karat gold medallion was taken and a fake chain left; a second car with at least two men blocked the couple's car. Husband 86, wife 75. Police confirmed arrests the following Monday (NBC Bay Area, Aug 23–24, 2026).
- Bellevue PD "blessing" thefts: Rickey Foreman, 71, at a Renton Walmart: "was clamping it around my neck"; the necklace held three rings, one his brother's, a Tuskegee Airman; recovered. Three arrested, a fourth charged; not-guilty pleas. In a linked Lynnwood H-Mart attempt a woman was knocked down and broke a vertebra (FOX 13 Seattle, Feb 13, 2026).
- Sacramento County Sheriff: jewelry distraction thefts at or near Costco, Sam's Club, Walmart, Lowe's, Home Depot and two Asian markets; targets mostly 50 to 70-plus; some victims followed home; "never allow strangers to place jewelry, scarves or other items around their necks" (Folsom Times, Jul 27, 2026).
- Vallejo Costco: a camera on a nearby car caught a woman knocked down from behind while loading and her purse taken; two minors arrested, tied to a second robbery an hour later (NBC Bay Area, Aug 21, 2025).
- Portland: three men followed a 93-year-old out of Costco and took his wallet; about $1,500 in gift cards at Fred Meyer before he got home; Det. Jordan Zaitz: "They're pretty organized"; attempts at Costcos in Portland, Tigard, Kirkland, Puyallup, Tukwila and Lynnwood (KOIN, May 24, 2024).
- Clarksville, TN Sam's Club, Jan 8, 2026: an 82-year-old's purse snatched as she returned her cart; three arrested (Clarksville Now, Jan 15, 2026).
- Boynton Beach, FL: police say two women in disguises stole wallets from older shoppers, then bought $100 gift cards; charges include "scheme to defraud a person over 65" (CBS12, Mar 12, 2026).
- Gold-for-gas cases: Kauai PD, Walmart lot, Feb 2025, arrest (county press release); Eugene PD, three victims in two weeks (KLCC, Mar 3, 2026); Hillsboro PD, Lowe's lot, Aug 2025, arrest (KPTV); West Shore RCMP, two arrested Mar 7, 2026.
- Auburn, AL: an iPhone alert; first ping at Sam's Club; a magnetic GPS tracker with audio under the car (WTVM, Sept 15, 2025).
- Costco's "Preventing Fraud" page (published 03/16/2026) lists six online and phone scams and nothing about the lot.
- Tracker alert paths: iPhone, Settings > Notifications > Tracking Notifications (plus Bluetooth and Location Services on), and Find My > Items > Unknown Items Detected with You ([Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119874)); Android, Settings > Safety & emergency > Unknown tracker alerts > Scan now ([Google Support](https://support.google.com/android/answer/13658562)).
- Boone County, WV Sheriff, Nov 1, 2026: trunk purchases before you arrive, park in busy, lit spots, lock every time (WTAP).
