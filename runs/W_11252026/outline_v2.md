*Airdate Wed Nov 25, 2026 · tapes Fri Nov 20 · A only (one long story, four scams) · no guest · 1 break · Stage: videos*

## SUMMARY

The most dangerous minute of a shopping trip is in the parking lot: arms full, trunk open, purse in the cart. Police say organized crews work store lots from Costco to Walmart to Sam's Club with four moves: a "gift" necklace that swaps out your real gold, a purse grabbed while you load and cards drained before you get home, a car that suddenly won't start, and a tracker stuck under your car. Four scams, each with its tell, then five moves that protect you every time you load the car.

## HERE'S HOW TO PROTECT YOURSELF
`A9 · the fix`

1. **PURSE IN THE CAR FIRST… THEN LOCK THE DOORS WHILE YOU LOAD** (the one thing from the tease)
   - Before a single bag goes in the trunk, your purse or wallet goes inside the car, and the doors get locked.
   - Never leave a purse in the cart or on the seat while your back is turned.
2. **NO STRANGER PUTS ANYTHING ON YOU, OR LOADS YOUR CAR**
   - Not a necklace, not a scarf, not a hug, not a "blessing." Step back and say no.
   - Leave the good jewelry at home, or tuck it in when you shop. And if someone grabs for it, let it go.
3. **WALLET GONE? CALL YOUR CARD COMPANIES BEFORE YOU LEAVE THE LOT**
   - They're buying gift cards within minutes. Every minute counts.
   - Turn on purchase alerts on every card so you see a charge the second it happens.
4. **CAR WON'T START? STAY INSIDE AND LOCK THE DOORS**
   - Call roadside assistance or police yourself. Never let a stranger work on your car.
5. **TURN ON TRACKER ALERTS ON YOUR PHONE** *(demo)*
   - iPhone: Settings, Notifications, Tracking Notifications, on. Bluetooth and Location Services have to be on too.
   - Android: Settings, Safety and emergency, Unknown tracker alerts. Tap Scan now for a check anytime.
   - Find one? Don't drive home. Go somewhere busy and call police.

## The fix: sources

- Step 1: purse in the car, lock the doors while loading (King County Sheriff via KIRO 7, Jun 24, 2019; Portland PD via KOIN, May 24, 2024); trunk purchases, lock every time (Boone County, WV Sheriff via WTAP, Nov 2, 2026).

- Step 4: stay in your locked car and call police (Culpeper County Sheriff via Fredericksburg Free Press, Aug 10, 2025); never let strangers work on your car (American Fork PD via ABC4, Jun 1, 2025).
- Step 5: iPhone Tracking Notifications, Bluetooth and Location Services ([Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119874)); Android Unknown tracker alerts and Scan now ([Google Support](https://support.google.com/android/answer/13658562)); report it to police (WTVM, Opelika investigators). ⚠️ "Don't drive home, go somewhere busy" needs a named police source (SPD's Sarah Lawson, KIRO 7, Nov 2023, is the closest).
