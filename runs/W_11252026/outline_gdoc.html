HERE'S HOW TO PROTECT YOURSELF

A9 · the fix

  1. PURSE IN THE CAR FIRST… THEN LOCK THE DOORS WHILE YOU LOAD (the one thing from the tease) - Before a single bag goes in the trunk, your purse or wallet goes inside the car, and the doors get locked. - Never leave a purse in the cart or on the seat while your back is turned.
  2. NEVER LET A STRANGER PUT ANYTHING ON YOU - Not a necklace, not a scarf, not a hug, not a "blessing." Step back and say no. - Leave the good jewelry at home, or tuck it in when you shop.
  3. SAY NO TO HELP YOU DIDN'T ASK FOR - A stranger insisting on loading your groceries is the setup, not a favor. - Never buy jewelry, gift cards or anything else in a parking lot. Walk away before money changes hands.
  4. WALLET GONE? CALL YOUR CARD COMPANIES BEFORE YOU LEAVE THE LOT - They're buying gift cards within minutes. Every minute counts. - Turn on purchase alerts on every card so you see a charge the second it happens.
  5. CAR WON'T START? STAY INSIDE AND LOCK THE DOORS - Call roadside assistance or police yourself. Never let a stranger work on your car.
  6. TURN ON TRACKER ALERTS ON YOUR PHONE (demo) - iPhone: Settings, Notifications, Tracking Notifications, on. Bluetooth and Location Services have to be on too. - Android: Settings, Safety and emergency, Unknown tracker alerts. Tap Scan now for a check anytime. - Find one? Don't drive home. Go somewhere busy and call police.
