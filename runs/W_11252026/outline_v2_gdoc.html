11/25 WED — THE PARKING LOT SCAMS

Airdate Wed Nov 25, 2026 · tapes Fri Nov 20 · A only (one long story, four scams) · no guest · 1 break · Stage: videos

SUMMARY

The most dangerous minute of a shopping trip is in the parking lot: arms full, trunk open, purse in the cart. Police say organized crews work store lots from Costco to Walmart to Sam's Club with four moves: a "gift" necklace that swaps out your real gold, a purse grabbed while you load and cards drained before you get home, a car that suddenly won't start, and a tracker stuck under your car. Four scams, each with its tell, then five moves that protect you every time you load the car.

HERE'S HOW TO PROTECT YOURSELF

A9 · the fix

  1. PURSE IN THE CAR FIRST… THEN LOCK THE DOORS WHILE YOU LOAD (the one thing from the tease) - Before a single bag goes in the trunk, your purse or wallet goes inside the car, and the doors get locked. - Never leave a purse in the cart or on the seat while your back is turned.
  2. NO STRANGER PUTS ANYTHING ON YOU, OR LOADS YOUR CAR - Not a necklace, not a scarf, not a hug, not a "blessing." Step back and say no. - Leave the good jewelry at home, or tuck it in when you shop. And if someone grabs for it, let it go.
  3. WALLET GONE? CALL YOUR CARD COMPANIES BEFORE YOU LEAVE THE LOT - They're buying gift cards within minutes. Every minute counts. - Turn on purchase alerts on every card so you see a charge the second it happens.
  4. CAR WON'T START? STAY INSIDE AND LOCK THE DOORS - Call roadside assistance or police yourself. Never let a stranger work on your car.
  5. TURN ON TRACKER ALERTS ON YOUR PHONE (demo) - iPhone: Settings, Notifications, Tracking Notifications, on. Bluetooth and Location Services have to be on too. - Android: Settings, Safety and emergency, Unknown tracker alerts. Tap Scan now for a check anytime. - Find one? Don't drive home. Go somewhere busy and call police.
