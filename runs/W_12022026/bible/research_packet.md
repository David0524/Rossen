## Traps

- **Details the outline adds that the source doesn't say:** "Washington, D.C." is the page's claim, not a place the boy lives. The outline's tease "He never said a word of it" is fine (the man says it's not him). "Grandpa" is the outline's word for Charles; no source says he has grandchildren. Use it only as a header word if at all; in lines, "an 84-year-old" / "this man."
- **Accused, not convicted:** nobody in this show is charged. Don't invent arrests. The dog scammers "weren't caught."
- **AI is a belief in most cases:** Jaden (an expert suspects), the Florida mom (she believes), the Massachusetts call (the daughter believes), the Texas dad (investigators say scammers can do this; not proven in his case). Only the fake-shop owners (ABC: "generated by artificial intelligence"), the dog photo (station calls it AI-generated) and the Burton videos (police report, "AI-generated") are stated as AI by the reporting.
- **Named companies:** George's Caps answered ABC (fact 8): Jeff note, not copy. Coinme said its kiosks display warnings and the funds were removed within minutes: Jeff note. TikTok removed the Jaden accounts (fact 4); TikTok told Charles his reports didn't break its rules, and didn't answer InvestigateTV (fact 11): Jeff notes.
- **Outcomes:** Florida mom: lost $15,000, refused the second $30,000. Texas dad: $15,000, unrecoverable. Dog couple: $1,900; dog safe. Burton case: $81,000+ and the condo sold; $70,000 more stopped. The condo money itself: the report says she sold it and was scheduled to send $70,000 of it; don't say she lost the condo money.
- **Advice that fails the target:**
  - The "safe word" alone fails people who haven't set one yet: the call-back move has to come first.
  - "Call them back" means the number already in your phone, never the number that called.
  - Step 3 must stay scoped to someone in trouble; plenty of real people use Zelle and cash.
  - The credit card line: a dispute right, not a guaranteed refund (fact 22).
  - Page transparency: a newer page isn't proof of a scam by itself. The outline's test is the mismatch, an old "family business" with a brand-new page.
- **Words the copy must not use:** "he doesn't exist" about Jaden; the mother's diagnosis; any victim surname; "Abigail" (one-off name); the scam shop's web address; "the tell"; any outlet name in a spoken line, including clip lead-ins (Jeff, 11/11; the red source line carries it).
- **Old cases (date in a JEFF note; copy stays evergreen):** Pleasant Green Apr 2025; Charles Sep 2025; Florida mom Jul 2025; Texas dad Sep 2025; Burton Aug 2025; dog Jan 2026; GMA shops May 2026; Jaden Jul 2026. Massachusetts Nov 2026 is current. No holidays, Giving Tuesday, Black Friday or "this season" in copy.
