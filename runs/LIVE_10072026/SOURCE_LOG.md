## Confirmed (primary or multiple sources)

- **Target basket beacon.** Snopes confirmed that Target tracks shopper locations with a basket-mounted Bluetooth beacon. Target's privacy policy cites LED light and Bluetooth tracking. Snopes says Target has used beacons since at least 2015 and that the device is from Acuity Brands. https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/target-basket-trackers/
- **TikTok view count.** 3.4M views, 392K likes, 11.8K comments (WeGotThisCovered, Mary Sue). BroBible later reported 3.7M+. **Pull the live count on show day.**
- **Lowe's cameras.** Lowe's privacy statement (updated March 11, 2026) says its cameras analyze which aisles customers visit and how long they stay, capture video, audio and images, and that image matching is used in some states after incidents. https://www.lowes.com/l/about/privacy-and-security-statement
- **Caper Carts.** Basket-facing cameras, outward-facing cameras and location tracking, per Instacart's June 4, 2026 Weis release. Deployed in 100+ cities across 15 states, including ShopRite, Wegmans and Weis (Instacart release; Modern Retail). Instacart says the cameras only recognize products (Retail Insider).
- **Instacart study.** 437 volunteers. Up to 23% higher prices for the same item. Lucerne eggs at a D.C. Safeway at five prices, from $3.99 to $4.79. About $1,200 a year for a family of four. Instacart ended item price tests on Dec 22, 2025. Sources: Consumer Reports press releases, AP/PBS, NBC, Reuters.
- **Maryland HB 895.** Protection From Predatory Pricing Act, signed April 28, 2026, effective October 1, 2026. Covers stores of 15,000+ sq ft and third-party delivery services. Penalties up to $10,000 per violation and $25,000 for repeat offenders. Enforced only by the attorney general, with a 45-day cure period. Loyalty-program pricing is exempt. Sources: Hunton, Skadden, Kiplinger.
- **FTC.** Proposed Enforcement Policy Statement on personalized pricing, Aug 19, 2026. It is not a rule, and the FTC says it lacks authority to ban personalized pricing in all cases. The chairman's line is **paraphrased** in the doc, not quoted. https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/08/ftc-seeks-comment-enforcement-policy-statement-regarding-personalized-pricing
- **Connecticut.** Disclosure requirement for personalized algorithmic pricing, effective October 1, 2026 (Holland & Knight).
- **Prime Big Deal Days.** Runs October 6–7, 2026 (aboutamazon.com).
