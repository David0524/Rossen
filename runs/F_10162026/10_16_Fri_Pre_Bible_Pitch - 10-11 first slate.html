THE HOOK

On Sept 7, Gamers Nexus and outside researchers published a two-hour investigation alleging LG TVs recorded room audio in standby, kept voice logs as plain text, and sent location data to LG's ad company. LG says the claims "are not true" and that audio is deleted unless you say the wake word. Nobody independent has settled it. The why-now chain: the investigation went viral → viewers are asking us whether their TV is listening (seven help-line calls) → what isn't disputed is the tracking: a feature called ACR, on by default, that checks what's on your screen. Texas forced Samsung (Feb 26) and LG (May 11) to ask permission, but only in Texas. Everyone else has to switch it off themselves. The listening is disputed. The watching isn't.

THE NUMBERS

  • 49 million LG smart TVs in the US (LG Ad Solutions' own ad pitch; "216 million" is the global figure).
  • Texas AG: ACR "can capture screenshots… every 500 milliseconds."
  • University researchers (2024) clocked LG sending data about every 15 seconds, Samsung about once a minute.
  • Vizio paid the FTC $2.2M in 2017 for tying viewing data to households' age and income.

FOOTAGE WE NEED

  • The get: real TVs on set, or screen recordings of each brand's menu. That's the segment.
  • Gamers Nexus and She Prepares (creator clips, clearance needed); our own January video.
  • ⚠️ No local news clip over 200K exists. This is a demo segment, not a clip segment.

THE PAYOFF

  • The one setting: turn off ACR. Samsung: Viewing Information Services. LG: Viewing Information (Live Plus). Vizio: Viewing Data. Roku TVs: Use Info from TV Inputs. Sony: Samba Interactive TV. (demo; verify on 2026 sets)
  • Mic: Roku, set Microphone Access to Never Allow. Samsung has a physical mic switch underneath. (verify)
  • Also turn off interest-based or cross-device ads. Your TV keeps working.

THE HOOK

An 86-year-old Houston customer treated her Frost Bank teller "like daughter." Police say the teller made six withdrawals from her IRA, about $180,000, until $1.50 was left, then claimed she'd carried the cash to the woman's car. The bank repaid her (KTRK, Dec 2023). On Oct 9, 2026, a former Santander employee in Rhode Island got 30 months for taking more than $125,000 from a 78-year-old with dementia. The same pattern turns up in Michigan (PNC, about $80,000), Hawaii (about $44,000, two elderly customers), and Georgia (Regions, $8,000). Then the phone store: Houston police say a clerk sent herself $400 from an elderly customer's Cash App while "setting up" a new phone.

HOW IT WORKS

  • They know you, your balance, and that you don't read statements.
  • Withdrawal slips you didn't sign; cash that never leaves the vault.
  • Extended threat: you hand your unlocked phone to a clerk and step away. Every money app on it is open.

  • Turn on a text alert for every withdrawal, any amount. Tell after the 86-year-old: six withdrawals, no alerts.

  • Never hand over your unlocked phone and walk away. Stand there and watch, or sign out of money apps first.
  • Ask your bank to add a trusted contact. (verify which banks offer this)

THE HOOK

On Oct 6 the Energy Information Administration said homes heating with electricity will spend about 4% more this winter, and heating oil homes about 21% more. Residential electricity prices rose 7.1% in 2025 (Consumer Reports, federal data). A Manassas, Virginia homeowner's January bill hit $281, up from about $100. Regulators' own market monitor puts data centers' cost to the 67 million customers in the PJM grid at about $29 billion. The House voted 417–3 in September to make big data centers pay their own way; the Senate blocked it. Why now: bills rise → people look for a cheaper rate → the supplier call. Illinois' attorney general settled with Rushmore Energy in June, alleging it enrolled people without consent at rates "nearly double" the utility's and claimed ties to the utility or a government "price protection program." The bill is real. The "program" isn't.

THE PAYOFF

  • Ask for budget billing; apply for LIHEAP through your state now. (verify 2026–27 status)

STORY 1 — SMART TV TRACKING AND LISTENING

Confirmed:

  • Gamers Nexus investigation, posted Mon Sept 7, 2026, with Level1Techs and researchers. Allegations: room audio recorded in standby; unmatched wake-word audio stored as plain text and uploaded later; network and Wi-Fi scanning; location sent to LG's ad company (Al Jazeera 9/10/2026: aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/10/lg-defends-smart-tv-features-amid-audio-surveillance-allegations; Decrypt 9/7/2026: decrypt.co/377637/lg-tv-might-be-listening-even-when-off; PPC Land 9/12/2026).
  • LG: "The claims made in the recently published video are not true." Audio "is immediately deleted if the wake word is not recognised." Standby listening only if Far-Field is on (Al Jazeera; Decrypt; CE Pro 9/16/2026). LG confirms network scanning is "a standard function."
  • 49M US / 216M global: LG Ad Solutions factsheet (lgads.tv/exclusive-factsheet/).
  • Texas AG suits filed 12/15/2025 (Sony, Samsung, LG, Hisense, TCL); "every 500 milliseconds" is from that release. Samsung agreement 2/26/2026, LG agreement 5/11/2026: consent required, opt-out, no fine stated. Sony, Hisense and TCL ongoing (texasattorneygeneral.gov).
  • IMC 2024 study (UC3M, UCL, UC Davis): LG about every 15 seconds, Samsung about every minute; tracking continued when the TV was used as an HDMI monitor (EurekAlert 12/10/2024).
  • Vizio FTC settlement, $2.2M, 11M TVs, demographic matching (FTC 2/6/2017).
  • Menu paths: Consumer Reports, updated 10/19/2025; Vizio's own viewing-data page.
  • Our coverage: "Your TV Is Spying On You — How to Stop It Immediately" (X-Lw0lcfVs8, ~Jan 2026, ~654K); "Your TV Is Spying on You — How to Stop it NOW" (VtliBJ7lfMc, ~spring 2026, ~316K); "Sony, Samsung & LG Sued" (Q1SzSm91W2k, 174K); brand-by-brand shorts. Help line: callers 334 and 214 plus five similar.
