paid in full. That is the beat end to end, from the person it happened to, and

## Four things to check before air

1. **The clip never says "A-to-Z Guarantee."** She says "appealed" and "denied."
   Local news effectively never uses Amazon's brand name for the process. Jeff's
   setup carries the term; the clip carries the experience. Fit scored 9, not 10.
2. **Her escalation was a TV station calling Amazon**, not the written escalation
   the script recommends. Adjacent, not identical — worth a beat of thought on
   "PUT IT IN WRITING."
3. **She paid by debit card.** The script does not mention it and it materially
   weakens dispute rights. Do not let the setup imply a credit card.
4. **Whisper renders "appealed" as "peeled."** Transcription artifact; the audio
   is correct. Do not quote the transcript file.

## The 60% stat in the setup

Not verified by this clip and not something the clip can support. Amazon's own
reporting has put third-party share of units sold above 60% for several years,
but if that line is going on air it should be sourced to a specific Amazon
disclosure with a date, not to this package.
