## RANK 1 — "The call showed your bank's real number. It wasn't your bank." (spoofed caller ID + the top Google link is a trap)

**Beat:** Your phone shows your bank's real number, or even the FBI's, and the first bank link on Google can be a scam ad. An Alabama woman did everything "right" and ended up with $13 to her name.

**What happened:**
- **Troy, AL: Alisha Watford.** She got a call that showed Wells Fargo's official number, the same one listed on the bank's website and app. The caller said her account had been flagged for fraud and she gave them the information they asked for. Her accounts were drained, leaving her $13, and no money has been recovered. WSFA story by Emma Ellis, published Sep 12–13, 2026.
  - Her quote: "you look up the number, it's the number that's on the Wells Fargo app."
  - Alabama Securities Commission director Amanda Senn: "Ninety-nine percent of the time we're not able to [recover funds]."
  - https://www.wsfa.com/2026/09/13/dont-rely-number-your-screen-phone-scam-drains-alabama-womans-savings/
- **FBI Boston, about Aug 19, 2026.** Scammers spoof FBI Boston's main number, 857-386-2000. The call starts with a fake "bank" rep who says your identity was used to buy illegal guns. You're transferred to a fake agent, the call "disconnects," and a callback shows the real FBI number. Then they push you onto an encrypted app. Massachusetts lost $24,508,041 in 706 government-impersonation cases in 2025. SAC Ted Docks is quoted.
  - https://fallriverreporter.com/fbi-boston-warning-public-of-new-scam-as-massachusetts-residents-lose-over-24-million/
  - https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/08/19/metro/fbi-agent-impersonation-scam-fraud-boston/ (search result only, not loaded)
- **FBI Albany** issued a similar warning about 518-465-7551. The fbi.gov page returned 403, so its date is not verified.
- **FBI IC3 PSA I-091726-PSA, Sep 17, 2026.** Nearly 61,000 complaints and more than $1.6B in losses from law-enforcement and government impersonation between Jan 2025 and Jul 2026. Scammers "spoof authentic phone numbers." Payment runs through couriers, crypto kiosks and wires, and AI-generated video calls are now in use. https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2026/PSA260917
- **The Google-ad side (DOJ, Sep 8, 2026).** Russian web developer Sergei Filimonov, 36, was extradited from Georgia (NDGA case). The ring bought sponsored Google and Bing links that looked like real banks and led to fake login pages. Its database held more than 5,000 stolen logins. DOJ puts attempted losses at about $28M and actual losses at about $14.6M, from at least 19 identified victims. He faces up to 175 years. The FBI's advice: bookmark your bank or open its app, and never click a search ad to log in.
  - https://www.wfmd.com/2026/09/20/scammers-are-buying-google-ads-to-steal-bank-logins/
  - https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndga/pr/russian-national-extradited-united-states-bank-account-takeover-fraud-scheme-causing (search result only)

**Named people on camera:** Alisha Watford (victim, WSFA). Keira Leann (TikTok victim, see below). Amanda Senn (ASC), probably in the WSFA package, though that's not confirmed.

## RANK 3 — "Someone sold your house while you were still in it" (home title theft)

**Beat:** Grandma's house was quietly signed over to a person who doesn't exist, then sold, and strangers are living in it now. Paid-off homes are the target.

**What happened:**
- **Harris County, TX, Sep 9, 2026.** County Attorney Abbie Kamin filed civil suits against nine people. They allegedly used forged signatures, fake identities, and notarized pages peeled off unrelated documents to transfer deeds. At least 10 homes are linked and about 7 owners are locked out. One family learned from county investigators that their late grandmother's home, where her son still lived, had been deeded to a fictional identity and sold. Kamin: "They are tricking notaries into signing paperwork…" https://gmg-kprc-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/news/local/2026/09/09/could-someone-steal-your-home-without-you-knowing-harris-county-warns-of-deed-fraud-schemes/
- **New Jersey SCI report, Sep 10, 2026.** At least 135 deed-fraud incidents from 2020 to Mar 2026, which the SCI says is second only to Florida per the FBI. Shore homes, seasonal homes and vacant land are most exposed. The state has few ID-check requirements for agents. https://www.nj.gov/sci/investigative-reports/approved/Deed-Fraud-and-Related-Schemes.shtml (via search) and https://newjersey.news12.com/2026/09/13/nj-ranks-among-top-states-for-reported-deed-fraud-as-investigators-find-gaps-in-safeguards/393heas4bYhC9i9Z7NGBwh (search result, not loaded)
- **Viral headline, Sep 10, 2026:** "FBI issues warning to US homeowners with 100% paid-off mortgages." It rests on a June 16 FBI alert and a July 23 D. Mass. case: three people charged in a multi-state scheme that took about $1.5M by selling vacant, lien-free properties. https://finance.yahoo.com/real-estate/articles/fbi-issues-warning-us-homeowners-105500748.html
- **Scale:** The FBI's 2024 report shows 9,359 real-estate fraud complaints and $173.6M in losses. Seniors filed 19% of complaints but took 44% of the losses. https://www.housingwire.com/articles/fbi-warns-of-home-title-theft-surge/ (search snippet)

**Named people:** Abbie Kamin (official). The Harris County family is not named in the text I loaded.

## RANK 4 — "Your doctor's app is texting you about a free Medicare kit. Your doctor didn't send it." (MyChart phishing)

**Beat:** Scammers dress up as MyChart, the portal you use to see your test results. They dangle a free "Medicare kit," then ask for a "shipping fee" and your card.

**What happened:**
- Emails and texts titled things like "Your MyChart Medicare Kit Awaits!" lead to a fake MyChart survey with a countdown clock. The kit is "$0" but there's a shipping fee, and the site takes personal and card data. A variant shows fake lab results and tells you to press keys that install malware.
- Epic says no such kit exists. At least 41 health systems have warned patients, including Penn Medicine and Jefferson. The FTC has 166 MyChart-related complaints (via Gizmodo FOIA). The PA AG issued a warning. https://healthjournalism.org/blog/2026/09/mychart-phishing-scam (Sep 16, 2026)
- CBS Chicago, Sep 23, 2026: Northwestern Medicine is warning patients. Jake Braun: "one wouldn't assume that their doctor would be the source of a scam." https://www.cbsnews.com/chicago/news/scammers-impersonating-mychart-to-steal-personal-information-install-malware/

**Named people:** Jake Braun (expert). Trevor Berceau of Epic is quoted in print. **No victim found.**
