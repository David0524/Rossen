## 1. "Your TV is listening even when it's off" (LG standby audio)
**Beat:** The TV in your bedroom may be recording the room while the screen is black, and it sends the audio out the next time it goes online.

**What happened:**
- Gamers Nexus, with Level1Techs and outside security researchers, published a 500+ hour, ~$70,000 investigation. They found current LG OLED TVs (for example the G5) recording microphone audio while in standby. With Ethernet unplugged the TV stored the recordings locally, then sent them once reconnected. The TVs also scanned home Wi-Fi for phones, PCs and printers. Installed base cited: ~216 million TVs. Sources: https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/privacy/2026/09/lg-tv-flaws-could-let-attackers-listen-in-even-in-standby-mode (Sep 7, 2026) and search summaries of https://startupfortune.com/gamers-nexus-says-216-million-lg-smart-tvs-are-secretly-listening-in-standby/ and https://lynnwoodtimes.com/2026/09/08/lg-smart/
- Search summaries also say mics kept capturing up to ~10 s after the user stopped talking, and that transcriptions were stored in plain text. I saw this only in search snippets (explainx/ynet), not on a loaded page, so treat it as unverified.
- **LG's response (Sep 10):** LG said "the claims made in the recently published video are not true." It says voice data is processed only when the user holds the mic button, or when an enabled far-field "Hi LG" wake word is recognized. Otherwise the audio is processed locally and deleted immediately. Network scanning is standard, and ACR needs consent. Sources: Malwarebytes update above; https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/10/lg-defends-smart-tv-features-amid-audio-surveillance-allegations (search result only)
- Gamers Nexus answered with a rebuttal video, "LG Says We're Fake News."
- **Legal:** Migliaccio & Rathod opened an investigation on Sep 9 (https://classlawdc.com/2026/09/09/lg-smart-tv-audio-recording-investigation/, search result). I did not confirm that any class action has been filed.
- **What to do:** Settings > Privacy & Terms / User Agreements. Turn off ACR/viewing information, personalized ads and voice recognition (Malwarebytes).

**Named people on camera:** Steve Burke (Gamers Nexus host). I inferred him from the channel; I did not confirm him from a page. Level1Techs (Wendell). No consumer victim.

## 2. "Your driver's license, with your face, is for sale on the dark web" (IDScan)
**Beat:** You handed your license to a bouncer, a dispensary or a rental counter. The scan (front, back and photo) ended up in a searchable dark-web store alongside 153 million others.

**What happened:**
- On Aug 31, 2026 a dark-web ID-theft service called **Nexus** launched on the Russian forum Exploit. It offered 153M+ US/Canadian driver's licenses, 10M+ ID cards, 3M+ travel documents and 579,000+ medical cards, and was growing ~400,000 records a day. The site went down Sep 2 at 8:56 pm. The FBI's New Orleans office opened an investigation Sep 1. Source: https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/09/fbi-probes-service-selling-153m-drivers-licenses/
- Brian Krebs found **his own Virginia license** in it, as six image files (front, back, basic, infrared and UV scans) timestamped June 2025, which matched a trip. Named people whose licenses were in the data: **Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth**, an FBI assistant director, and researchers Zach Edwards and Larry Baldwin (Krebs).
- Krebs lists IDScan.net clients as Hertz, Planet13 dispensaries (CA/FL/IL/NV), Target, FedEx, Motorola Solutions, Jack Henry and Caesars Entertainment. IDScan does "more than 21 million verifications monthly, at more than 20,000 locations."
- IDScan (Louisiana) confirmed that hackers took data from its cloud: names, DL/government ID numbers and photos. It is notifying people and offering free credit monitoring. Sources: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/10/id-verification-giant-idscan-confirms-data-breach-with-more-than-150-million-drivers-licenses-stolen/ and https://therecord.media/idscan-data-breach-notice-drivers-licenses (notice dated Sep 4, per The Record. Krebs says Sep 8 and classaction.org says Sep 3, so the dates conflict and need checking).
- **Lawsuits:** Several were filed in days. One aggregator claims 9 suits, mostly in the Eastern District of Louisiana (https://www.zyphe.com/resources/news/idscan-data-breach-class-actions-september-2026 and https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/multiple-class-action-lawsuits/ — search results only, not loaded). The Record (loaded) confirms "several lawsuits."
- **What to do** (WBAY/ITRC, loaded): freeze credit; sign up for your state DMV's transaction alerts (e.g. WI eNotify); treat breach "notification" texts with suspicion and check that the URL ends in .gov; report to FTC/IC3/police; pull free credit reports. Letters "can take months," so act now. Source: https://www.wbay.com/2026/09/16/data-breach-id-verification-company-may-have-exposed-millions-drivers-license-images/
- **Protection hook for Rossen:** a license can't be changed like a password, and the scans include the photo, which feeds fake IDs, account takeovers and selfie-verification fraud. I did not find a sourced example of that fraud tied to this breach.

**Named people on camera:** James Lee, President of the Identity Theft Resource Center (WBAY, 9/16). Brian Krebs (no video of him found). **No ordinary victim found**. Nobody so far has said on camera "my license was in it and here's what happened."

## 3. Money-back roundup: Apple Siri $250M + CVS $20.5M (light C story)
**Beat:** Two checks you can claim this week: Apple for your iPhone, and CVS for using its app or website.

**Apple $250M.** **Correction to the brief:** this is **not** the Siri-eavesdropping case. It settles claims that Apple advertised Apple Intelligence/Siri AI features that were delayed.
- Who qualifies: US buyers of an iPhone 15 Pro/Pro Max or any iPhone 16 bought Jun 10, 2024 - Mar 29, 2025.
- Payout: $25 per device, possibly up to $95. Claims at SmartphoneAISettlement.com, Sep 21 - Dec 21, 2026. You need the serial number.
- Final approval hearing is Sep 29, 2027, so **no money for roughly a year or more**.
- Sources: https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/20/siri-ai-settlement-website-now-live/ and https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/21/you-can-now-claim-your-payout-from-apples-250-million-siri-settlement/ (search-result summaries; the "Sep 29, 2027" date looks odd, so verify on the settlement site).
- The older **$95M Lopez v. Apple** "Siri listened to private conversations" settlement is a separate case (WTHR clip below).

**CVS $20.5M.** Settles claims that CVS.com, CVSHealth.com and the CVS app shared user data with Criteo and other third parties.
- Who qualifies: anyone who used them in the US before Jul 27, 2026.
- Payout: up to $10 with proof, $5 without. Deadline **Nov 16, 2026**; final hearing Dec 1, 2026.
- Sources: https://www.claimdepot.com/settlements/cvs-digital-privacy-settlement, https://thehill.com/business/6104529-cvs-class-action-settlement-file-claim-payment/ and https://cvsdigitalprivacysettlement.com/ (search summaries).

## 4. "Your car is telling your insurance company how you drive" (weak peg)
**Beat:** Your car logged every hard brake and late-night trip and sold it, and your premium went up.

**Facts:**
- FTC finalized an order against GM/OnStar in Jan 2026: a 5-year ban on sharing driving/geo data with consumer reporting agencies, and consent required for 20 years. Location was collected as often as every 3 seconds. Source: https://www.michiganpublic.org/news/2026-01-16/general-motors-agrees-to-not-sell-driver-behavior-data-for-five-years-in-settlement-with-ftc (search result)
- California reached a $12.75M settlement with GM in May 2026, over data sold to Verisk and LexisNexis; GM earned ~$20M nationwide. Source: https://www.moneygeek.com/insurance/auto/driving-data-insurers-privacy/ (search summary)
- A federal judge let the GM/OnStar wiretap class action proceed in Apr 2026. Source: https://dicellolevitt.com/court-allows-gm-onstar-vehicle-data-privacy-lawsuit-to-move-forward/ (search result)
- The "258-page LexisNexis report, 640 trips" example comes from a NYT story from 2024, not new.
- **Nothing new found for Aug 15 - Sep 29, 2026.**
