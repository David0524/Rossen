### 1. "The airbag in your used car could be a bomb, and a recall check won't find it." (A-story contender)
**What happened**
- NHTSA links 11 deaths and 3 severe injuries across 14 crashes to counterfeit or substandard Chinese replacement airbag inflators marked **DTN60DB** (made by Jilin Province Detiannuo). Instead of cushioning the driver, they rupture and fire metal shrapnel into the chest, neck, eyes and face. https://www.goodcarbadcar.net/nhtsa-dtn60db-counterfeit-inflator-eleventh-death-salvage-channel/
- **New peg:** the 11th death happened **Aug 27, 2026 in Dallas**, in a 2018 Chevy Equinox. It's the first case outside the Malibu/Sonata models. NHTSA called it an otherwise survivable crash, and it came **after** NHTSA's April 29, 2026 forced-recall ban, which was the agency's first forced equipment recall order in 20+ years. https://hoodline.com/2026/09/dallas-driver-becomes-11th-us-death-tied-to-banned-chinese-air-bag-part/ (pub 9/4) ; https://carbuzz.com/counterfeit-chinese-airbag-death-september-2026/ (pub 9/9; says CBP seized 490 fake inflators in 2024, 10x the 2023 number)
- How it happens: salvage and theft repairs use ~$100 fake inflators in place of ~$1,000 genuine ones, and cars get retitled. **"A VIN lookup will return no recalls on an affected car"**, so only a physical inspection finds them. The identifier sequence is 144415654 666631 / 666633. NHTSA says owners of used cars that were in crashes since 2020 should get an inspection and shouldn't check the airbag themselves. Report suspect parts at 888-327-4236. https://www.goodcarbadcar.net/... (above) ; https://www.repairerdrivennews.com/2026/09/09/counterfeit-airbag-death-total-continues-to-uptick/

**Named people on camera / human victim**
- **Alexia De La Rosa, 17**, of West Valley City, Utah, was killed July 30, 2025 in a minor crash. She was driving a 2019 Hyundai Sonata her mother **Tessie** bought from AutoSavvy, and the inflator exploded "like a grenade." The family filed a wrongful-death suit (Morgan & Morgan). https://www.fox13now.com/news/local-news/west-valley/utah-teens-death-sparks-wrongful-death-lawsuit-over-allegedly-defective-air-bag ; https://www.repairerdrivennews.com/2026/03/05/family-files-suit-over-teen-killed-by-improper-repair-involving-counterfeit-airbag/
- Denver7 investigation: on camera are family attorney **Andrew Parker Felix** ("This is a fully survivable crash…") and CSP Sgt. Ivan Alvarado. It includes industry crash-test video. https://www.denver7.com/news/investigations/lawsuit-claims-autosavvy-sold-car-with-deadly-counterfeit-airbag
- The Dallas victim's name was not found.

### 2. "The USDA stamp on your meat was fake: 167,000 pounds sold nationwide."
**What happened**
- On Sept 22, 2026, FSIS announced **Star Meat Delivery Inc. (Lucama, NC)** was recalling ~**167,639 lbs** of raw pork, beef and goat. FSIS says the meat was produced without federal inspection and labeled with a **false USDA mark, "EST. 1363."** It was shipped to A&D Foods (Duluth, GA) and on to retailers and restaurants nationwide under the A&D Foods label. It's a **Class I** recall (reasonable probability of serious adverse health consequences or death). No illnesses reported. https://www.today.com/health/recall/massive-meat-recall-usda-inspection-false-label-rcna599435 ; https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/usda-meat-recall-inspection/ (not loaded, search result only)
- Products include goat cuts, sliced ribeye, chuck, oxtail, short ribs, shanks, beef feet, ground pork, chorizo, chops, pork feet, ribs, hock and menudo mix (Today).
- **The company disputes it.** Star Meat says EST. 1363 was legitimately reserved during its USDA application and the meat came from an inspected supplier. https://www.agdaily.com/livestock/north-carolina-meat-distributor-disputes-false-usda-stamp-claim-after-recall/ (search-result summary)
- Pattern: a similar fake-establishment-number recall hit Sabrositos Hondurenos' "OLANCHO" chorizo (32,000 lbs, "Est. 1785"), dated Aug 20, **2025** per Yahoo. https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/32-000-pounds-meat-recalled-155306350.html

**Named people on camera:** no consumers or victims found. Possible company spokesperson (Star Meat statement).
