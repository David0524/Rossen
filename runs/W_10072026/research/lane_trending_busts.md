### 1. (A-story candidate) "The 'federal agent' at the door wants your gold": the $250M gold-courier ring, and the ringleader caught at JFK

**Sexy beat:** Scammers told more than 200 seniors their bank was hacked, had them buy gold bars "for safekeeping," sent a courier to their door to collect it, then melted it into jewelry. The man police call a central figure was caught boarding a flight out of the country.

**What happened (facts + sources):**
- **Sept 3–4, 2026:** Tarrant County (TX) DA Phil Sorrells announced the arrest of **Muzamil Ahmed, 35**, of New Jersey, at JFK. He was waiting to board a flight to Jeddah, Saudi Arabia. The ring has **40+ indictments, 200+ victims, more than $250M stolen** and **$130M+ in gold seized**. Two more arrests were made in Chicago and Newark. The calls came from overseas call centers, mainly in India. The gold was melted at a Florida refinery or made into jewelry, and some was turned into crypto. The ring has operated since 2018. One victim lost $2M. Sorrells said at least one victim died by suicide.
  - FOX 4: https://www.fox4news.com/news/tarrant-county-gold-theft-scheme-arrests
  - CBS Texas: https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/tarrant-county-senior-fraud-ring-muzamil-ahmed-arrest-september-2026/
  - NBC DFW: https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/local/tarrant-county-investigation-scam-older-adults/4073029/
  - Moneywise/Yahoo, 9/8/2026 (Ahmed indicted Aug 28; tied to at least 35 victims individually): https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/crypto/articles/over-200-us-seniors-hand-091500007.html
- **National scale:** the FBI's IC3 logged about **725 gold-courier complaints in 2025 with $311.8M lost**, roughly $400K per complaint. In 2024 it was $219M across 525 complaints. That's the same Yahoo/Moneywise article citing IC3 data. I couldn't load the FBI page itself (403), so treat these as secondhand.
  - Overall IC3 figure for people 60+ in 2025: **201,000+ complaints and $7.7B lost**, up 59%. https://www.housingwire.com/articles/fbi-seniors-cybercrime-2025/ (seen in search results only)
- **The quotes:** "If you prey on Tarrant County seniors, we are coming for you. If you run, we will find you." (Sorrells, FOX 4). And: "Instead, he caught a flight to Tarrant County and landed in our jail." (Sorrells, CBS Texas).
- **Victim who fought back (Sept 15, 2026):** an elderly Frisco woman lost $30K to fake federal agents. When they came back for $22K more, a bank manager flagged it. Frisco police then ran a controlled delivery with counterfeit cash and arrested the courier. **She is anonymous** and asked not to be identified. https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/she-lost-30-000-fake-162113526.html (Wealth of Geeks, citing FOX 4)
- **Older bust footage from the same ring (Feb 2026):**
  - Collin County Sheriff's raids on Tilak Jewelers (Irving) and Saima Jewelers (Frisco).
  - A raid by 20 SWAT officers on Malani Jewelers (Richardson), with simultaneous raids in Atlanta and Orlando. "Crates full of gold" were seized, more than $50M recovered, 13 employees detained, and Texans' losses put at $74.5M. https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/gold-bar-scam-raid-richardson-jewelry-store/

**Named 55+ victims on camera:**
- **Robert Brown, 78**, a retired oil geologist from Little Elm, TX, lost $2M. He **died in November 2025** without getting the money back. His **daughter Katie Brown spoke on camera** to CBS Texas (story dated 2/3/2026): "It cleaned out his retirement savings." https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/north-texas-gold-bar-scam-elderly-victim-loses-2-million/
  - The Sept 2026 CBS Texas arrest story names him as one of this ring's victims.
  - Robert himself isn't on camera because he has died. His daughter is the on-camera human.
- The Frisco woman is anonymous. I found no other named on-camera victims tied to the September arrest.

### 2. "The Amazon of scams": a $24B marketplace, 13 scam compounds raided and 400 arrested, and the next hit is coming days before we air

**Sexy beat:** The fake investment site that texts you started out as a product. Crime bosses bought it off a Telegram "store" the way you'd order takeout. The feds just took the store down.

**What happened:**
- **Sept 9, 2026:** DOJ's Scam Center Strike Force (U.S. Attorney Jeanine Pirro, D.C.) and Treasury OFAC acted together against **Xinbi Guarantee**, a Chinese-language Telegram marketplace with **650,000+ users**.
  - Vendors on it custom-built fake investment-firm websites, recruited trafficking victims to work in scam compounds, and laundered victims' money.
  - Treasury says it processed about **$24B since 2022**.
  - **$52M** in crypto was restrained in one day. The Strike Force's running total is now about **$938M** frozen.
  - OFAC sanctioned Xinbi, 52 TRON wallets, and two app developers: Anwen Technology (Cambodia) and SafeW Technology (Singapore).
  - Pirro: "If Chinese organized crime can buy a custom website and money laundering service the way you order takeout, then every American with a retirement account is in the blast radius."
  - WSET: https://wset.com/news/nation-world/doj-justice-department-scam-center-strike-force-attorney-jeanine-pirro-takes-down-chinese-scam-network-xinbi-guarantee-telegram-targeting-every-american-with-a-retirement-account-treasury-sanctions-money-laundering-cryptocurrency
  - OFAC: https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20260909
  - DOJ/USSS release: https://www.secretservice.gov/newsroom/releases/2026/09/scam-center-strike-force-conducts-seizures-chinese-run-illicit-scammer
  - The DOJ release page itself loaded empty for me: https://www.justice.gov/usao-dc/pr/scam-center-strike-force-conducts-seizures-chinese-run-illicit-scammer-marketplace-and
- **Madagascar:** the Strike Force spent two weeks helping local authorities dismantle **13 Chinese-run scam compounds**. Nearly **400 arrested**, about 3,200 devices processed, and about 30 Chinese bosses repatriated to China. https://gulfnews.com/world/africa/us-says-it-helped-crack-madagascar-online-scam-centers-1.500669041 (from search results)

A **joint in-person disruption operation hosted by the NCA in London is scheduled for "early October."** https://www.justice.gov/usao-dc/pr/us-and-uk-launch-first-its-kind-joint-alliance-dismantle-global-scam-centers (seen in search results; IRS-CI mirror: https://www.irs.gov/compliance/criminal-investigation/us-and-uk-launch-first-of-its-kind-joint-alliance-to-dismantle-global-scam-centers). **Watch for news 10/1–10/6. ### 4. Fake lawyers promised seniors a timeshare settlement and took $36M: Operation Silver Shores guilty plea (9/14)

**Sexy beat:** A "lawyer" calls to say you're owed money from your old timeshare. All you have to do is pay the fees first.

**What happened:** On Sept 14, 2026, **Juliet Mora, 42**, pleaded guilty to money-laundering conspiracy (E.D. Cal.). The network posed as attorneys and paralegals promising timeshare-related settlements. **More than 400 elderly victims lost more than $36M.** Fifteen US-based members were arrested in Oct 2025 in CA, TX and FL. Mora was arrested at Boston Logan in Dec 2025, arriving from Panama. $2.1M has been seized. Sentencing is set for 12/14/2026.
- https://themoneyoverview.com/justice-says-a-september-plea-follows-a-2025-seizure-of-more-than-2-1-million-from-an-elderly-fraud-network/

**Named victims:** None.

**Clips:**
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P1tIaCmuk2Q (KMPH FOX26, 16:51, 734 views, inferred Oct 2025). Presser: "U.S. Attorney to announce arrests in telemarketing fraud targeting elderly."

**Takeaway:** A real settlement never charges you upfront fees. Check the "law firm" yourself, and call the timeshare company directly.
