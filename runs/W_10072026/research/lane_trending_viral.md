## RANK 1 — "She handed the scammer a box of fake cash. Police were waiting." (Frisco senior sting + $250M gold ring)

**New facts and clips for pass-1 "courier cash pickups."** This fixes that pitch's biggest weakness: it had no victim on camera. Now there is one.

**Beat:** A Texas grandmother lost $30K to "federal agents." When they came back for more, she worked with police, handed the courier a box of fake cash, and watched him get arrested. That courier led to a $250 million ring.

**What happened:**
- **The Frisco sting.** An elderly Frisco resident (name and age withheld) lost $30,000 to callers posing as government officials, who said her bank information was compromised.
  - When they asked for $22,000 more from a second bank, the bank manager told her it was a scam.
  - Frisco PD gave her counterfeit money for a sting, and the courier was arrested at the handoff.
  - Her quote: "I hand him the box with the money in it, and I see who it is, and he says, 'Blue!' and drives off, and the police catch him."
  - FOX 4, Sep 14, 2026: https://www.fox4news.com/news/elderly-frisco-resident-aids-authorities-busting-international-gold-theft-ring
  - Yahoo/FOX 4, Sep 15 (says the callers posed as the "Social Security Administration fraud division"): https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/texas-senior-helps-police-turn-211948505.html
- **The ring.** Muzamil Ahmed, 35, was arrested at JFK on Sep 4 while trying to leave the country. Scale:
  - more than 200 victims, $250M stolen, more than $130M in gold seized, more than 40 indictments
  - one victim lost $2M
  - Method: India-based call centers told seniors to turn their savings into gold bars or bitcoin. Fake federal agents picked it up, and the gold was melted at a Florida refinery.
  - Tarrant County DA Phil Sorrells: "If you prey on Tarrant County seniors, we are coming for you. If you run, we will find you."
  - FOX 4, Sep 4: https://www.fox4news.com/news/tarrant-county-gold-theft-scheme-arrests
- **The named $2M victim** (background, not fresh). Robert Brown, 78, a retired oil geologist from Little Elm, lost more than $2M. He died without recovering it. CBS Texas: https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/north-texas-gold-bar-scam-elderly-victim-loses-2-million/

- **A second fresh sting with fake gold: Hicksville, NY.** A 70-year-old woman wired **$919,200** (Jun 29 – Jul 27) after a caller claiming to be "Delhi Police" said she was tied to a crime in India.
  - Then she was told to buy $300K in gold bars and coins for a "CIA agent" to inspect.
  - She called police instead. Detectives ran a sting with fake gold in a Hicksville McDonald's parking lot, and the courier gave a code word before he was arrested.
  - Fnu Tierigeli, 32, of Queens was arrested Sep 2.

## RANK 2 — "That video promising seniors a property-tax freeze? It's fake. AI made it, and the phone number goes to scammers."

**Home angle. Not pitched in pass 1.**

**Beat:** AI-generated "news" videos tell homeowners over 65 that Congress just froze their property taxes. They give a form number, and it doesn't exist. Assessor offices in at least five states are flooded with calls.

**What happened:**
- **The claim.** Congress passed senior property-tax relief. Call your assessor, or a number given in the video, and ask for a "Senior Homeowner Tax Review Request" or "IRS Form 1098-SR." Neither exists.
  - Wisconsin DOR (Sep 3): "This is an AI-generated video designed to trick seniors into sharing Social Security numbers and banking details, or paying fees for nonexistent filing services." https://www.wispolitics.com/2026/dept-of-revenue-warns-senior-homeowners-about-viral-property-tax-scam/
- **Scale and spread:**
  - Realtor.com found "50 videos across 49 YouTube channels … 35 of which appeared within a span of just three days." Thirty were labeled AI.
  - At least 23 assessor and auditor offices issued warnings.
  - YouTube terminated at least one channel for spam.
  - Moneywise/Yahoo, Aug 16: https://finance.yahoo.com/real-estate/articles/50-ai-generated-youtube-videos-143000878.html
  - The Moneywise follow-up headline says "23 assessor offices": https://moneywise.com/taxes/property-tax-seniors-ai-youtube-scam-relief (403 for me; headline seen in search)
- **Official warnings:**
  - Los Angeles County, Sep 3. Assessor Jeff Prang: "There is no federal 1098-SR form that freezes or reduces your property taxes." https://lacounty.gov/2026/09/03/county-officials-warn-property-owners-about-fake-property-tax-relief-scam/
  - Sonoma County, Aug 19: https://sonomacounty.gov/county-assessor-and-tax-collector-warn-of-false-1098-sr-property-tax-claims-targeting-seniors
  - Hamilton County, OH, Aug 11. Axios describes a "30-minute" AI video: https://www.axios.com/local/cincinnati/2026/08/11/hamilton-county-auditor-warns-of-property-tax-scam
  - Riverside County (Village News, Sep 24): https://www.villagenews.com/story/2026/09/24/community/county-treasurer-larry-cohen-warns-homeowners-about-fake-property-tax-relief-scam-fraudulent-claims-promote-nonexistent-1098-sr-form-and-promise-to-reduce-or-freeze-property-taxes/82882.html
  - Colorado: Douglas County (Denver7) and 9NEWS
  - WIZM (La Crosse), Sep 15 (page wouldn't load for me)
- **Losses:** none reported. The documented harm is floods of calls and the risk of handing over SSNs and bank details.
