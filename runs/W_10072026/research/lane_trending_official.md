## RANK 1: "He said he was a federal agent. He came to her door for her gold."
**Beat.** Scammers in overseas call centers pose as the bank and then the feds. They tell seniors to turn their savings into gold bars and hand them to a "courier for safekeeping." In one Texas case the gold was melted down into jewelry.

**What happened (Sep 2026)**
- **Tarrant County, TX.** Muzamil Ahmed, 35, was arrested at JFK Airport while trying to leave the country. He is charged with organized theft, financial abuse of the elderly and money laundering.
  - Scale: more than 200 victims, **$250M stolen**, over $130M in gold seized, 40+ indicted. One victim lost $2M.
  - How the money moved: gold was melted at a Florida refinery into jewelry, and cash was converted to crypto and sent overseas. The call centers were mostly in India.
  - DA Phil Sorrells: "If you prey on Tarrant County seniors, we are coming for you. If you run, we will find you."
  - FOX 4, published 9/4/2026: https://www.fox4news.com/news/tarrant-county-gold-theft-scheme-arrests ; Hoodline 9/4 (ring operating since 2018): https://hoodline.com/2026/09/tarrant-county-nabs-fugitive-accused-of-running-250m-senior-gold-scam/
- **Frisco, TX victim sting (FOX 4, 9/14/2026).** An elderly woman lost $30K to a "federal agent." When the scammer came back for $22K more, her bank manager stepped in. Frisco PD then gave her counterfeit money to hand to the courier, and the courier was arrested. FOX 4's Lori Brown interviewed her on camera; she is **unidentified at her request**. https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/elderly-frisco-resident-aids-authorities-213532583.html
- **Pennsylvania State Police.**
  - Two people were arrested 9/10 after a traffic stop on I-80 in Union County. The car was a silver 2025 Honda Accord with California plates. PSP ties the pair to about **$6.5M** stolen.
  - How it worked: the contact started with **computer pop-ups** and fake federal agents. Victims were told to buy gold and give it to a courier.
  - PSP put out a public appeal on 9/28. Trooper Eric Dreisbach: "No legitimate law enforcement officer will instruct you to purchase gold…" https://www.bctv.org/2026/09/28/state-police-seek-publics-assistance-and-warn-public-of-gold-bar-scam/
  - Local21 reports one victim was a 74-year-old woman who lost more than $250K (search result only): https://local21news.com/news/local/two-people-arrested-for-gold-bar-scam-that-stole-over-250k-from-a-74-year-old-police-scam-fraud-crime-local-impact-pennsylvania-pa-impersonation-transactions-honda-police-officials-gold-bars-money-
- **South Whitehall, PA.** A 78-year-old woman handed over gold in four courier pickups, **$683K** in total. Jiwan Chen and Tianhang Zhou of New York were arrested; Chen was caught during a surveillance operation. Search result only: https://hoodline.com/2026/09/south-whitehall-senior-loses-683k-in-gold-to-ny-scam-duo-police-say/
- **NY AG Letitia James alert (8/7/2026).** The NYPD has investigated 100+ gold-bar cases in two years, with losses over $100M. The scam starts with a fake pop-up, then remote access, then a fake cop, then gold, then a courier. https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2026/attorney-general-james-warns-new-yorkers-gold-bar-scam-targeting-seniors
- **FBI IC3 PSA I-091726 (9/17/2026).** Nearly 61,000 complaints and **$1.6B** lost to law-enforcement or government impersonation from Jan 2025 to Jul 2026. Payment demands include "couriers… or cash inserted into cryptocurrency kiosks," and scammers now use AI-generated video calls. https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2026/PSA260917
- **FTC alert (Sept).** "The FTC will never… tell you to withdraw cash or buy gold and give it to someone." From the search result: https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts

**On camera.** The Frisco victim (anonymous, FOX 4). DA Phil Sorrells, at FOX 4 and CBS Texas pressers. No named victim so far.

## RANK 2: "The machine at the gas station that swallowed her life savings, and the states that are unplugging it"
**Beat.** Crypto ATMs in corner stores are how scammers take seniors' cash. Hawaii switches off cash deposits **Oct 1**, a week before air.

**What happened**
- **IC3 2025 kiosk data:**
  - About $389M lost across 13,400+ complaints.
  - Complaints up 23% and losses up 58% from 2024.
  - People 50+ made more than half the complaints and lost $302M.
  - Sen. Reed release, 9/1/2026: https://www.reed.senate.gov/news/releases/reed-its-time-to-pull-the-plug-on-crypto-atm-scams
- **Where the losses fall.** 60+ accounted for **76% of losses** where age was known. Laws: 35 states have passed kiosk laws since 2023, 18 of them in 2026. Indiana's ban was signed 3/9/2026. AARP, updated 9/14/2026: https://www.aarp.org/advocacy/crypto-atm-fraud-protections/
- **Hawaii.** From **10/1/2026** no one can deposit cash into a crypto kiosk. 2025 losses were $3.85M, about four times the year before. Banking Commissioner Dwight Young says kiosk users look "panicked, scared or worried… There's never a positive emotion from these people." https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com/2026/08/12/new-hawaii-law-banning-cryptocurrency-atm-deposits-take-effect-oct-1/
- **Other bans.** Tennessee (effective 7/1/2026) and Minnesota (effective 8/1/2026; machines must be removed by 12/31). https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/cryptocurrency/tennessee-bans-crypto-atms-that-have-become-payment-portal-of-choice-for-scammers-second-state-to-restrict-machines-after-indiana ; https://www.foxbusiness.com/politics/minnesotas-ban-crypto-atms-goes-effect-after-citizens-report-losing-nearly-1-million-scams (search results)
- **New victim, today (ABC15, 9/29).** An 87-year-old Mesa widow met a scammer on social media after she posted about her husband's death. Over seven months he steered her to crypto ATMs in corner and liquor stores and then to a $500K cashier's check. Mesa PD traced the blockchain and recovered nearly $200K. She was **not identified or shown**, but she is quoted: "All of a sudden, I was talking to a perfect stranger, and it was exciting." Det. Tim Letson of Mesa PD Financial Crimes is on camera. https://www.abc15.com/news/region-southeast-valley/mesa/an-87-year-old-mesa-woman-lost-500-000-to-an-online-romance-scammer
- **Hero clerk (WESH, updated 9/18).** Haroon Anwar and his daughter own a Mobil station on Sanford Ave in Seminole County, FL. Their family and staff have stopped more than $100K in scam deposits. Search result only: https://muckrack.com/olivia-tyler
- **Background.** The D.C. AG sued Athena Bitcoin, alleging 93% of deposits were scam-linked, the median victim age was 71, and hidden fees ran up to 26%. **This was filed 9/2025, not 2026**, so use it as background only. https://www.consumerfinanceinsights.com/2025/09/12/d-c-attorney-general-sues-crypto-atm-company/

## RANK 3: "Medicare 'enrollment' calls start now, and one call center faked seniors' voices saying yes"
**Beat.** Open Enrollment runs Oct 15 to Dec 7. The FTC says scammers are **buying the top search results** for Medicare. Separately, the feds say a call center **used AI to fabricate recordings of seniors "consenting"** to test kits.

**What happened**
- **FTC alert (9/28/2026).** Health-insurance scams surge during Open Enrollment. Scammers "pay to appear at the top" of search results, spoof Medicare.gov and Healthcare.gov look-alikes, and victims end up with fake plans or medical identity theft. The FTC held a healthcare-scams roundtable with CMS and Senior Medicare Patrol on **9/29/2026**. https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2026/09/what-know-ahead-open-enrollment-avoid-health-insurance-scams
- **HHS-OIG fugitives (Sept 2026).** Fizza Farid, Faizan Saleem, Shearyar Arif and Ruknuddin "Rick" Charolia allegedly ran "Hello International Marketing Solutions," a call center in Pakistan. It collected Medicare numbers through hacking, scraping, and fake "free health product" sites.
  - Scale: **$703,834,742** in false claims for COVID test kits, medical equipment and genetic tests (N.D. Illinois).
  - AI angle: several outlets report it **used AI to fabricate audio of beneficiaries consenting**.
  - Sources: https://www.jpost.com/international/article-907810 ; https://nayadaur.tv/10-Sep-2026/four-pakistanis-wanted-alleged-dollar-703-million-us-medicare-fraud-scheme ; HHS-OIG page https://oig.hhs.gov/fraud/fugitives/faizan-saleem/ (**returned 503 when I tried; confirm there**)
