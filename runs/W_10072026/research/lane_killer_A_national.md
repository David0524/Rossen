## RECOMMENDATION: "His caregiver took his house for $5, and the daughter got handcuffed."

**The beat:** You hire a nurse to care for Dad at home. Weeks later, the nurse has power of attorney and the deed to his house. When his daughter shows up, he has been dead for three days, the locks are changed, there are strangers' photos on the walls, and police put *her* in handcuffs.

**Why it's the A story:**
- It hits HOME, LIFE SAVINGS and BODY (alleged physical abuse, a Parkinson's patient) in one story.
- It has a named family member on camera who tells it herself.
- It has the biggest fresh 55+ clip I found anywhere on the national agenda: a WSMV Short at **5.68M views**.
- It has not aired. There are no caregiver, deed or title-theft titles in rossen_channel_titles_0929.txt, and no match for "quitclaim," "title theft," "deed fraud" or "caregiver" in the repo's scripts outside this run.
- **It fixes what killed "home title theft"** in pass 3, which was "no fresh victim video."

### What happened (sourced)
- **The father:** Robert Rowland, 75, of Conway, SC. He had Parkinson's disease and a brain injury and was classified as a vulnerable adult.
- **March 2024:** Nurse Sarah Smalls began caring for him through Griswold Home Care.
- **April 2024:** Smalls was named his power of attorney. On **Apr 17, 2024**, a quitclaim deed moved his house to her for **$5**. Bank records show transfers to her, including some after his death.
  - WSMV (Gray), 8/29/2026: https://www.wsmv.com/2026/08/29/this-is-my-dads-house-daughter-says-caregiver-took-deed-her-fathers-home-weeks-before-his-death/
  - Original station, WMBF, 8/27: https://www.wmbfnews.com/2026/08/27/this-is-my-dads-house-daughter-claims-nurse-took-deed-fathers-conway-home-before-his-death/
  - Also syndicated on KBTX: https://www.kbtx.com/2026/08/29/this-is-my-dads-house-daughter-says-caregiver-took-deed-her-fathers-home-weeks-before-his-death/
- **The discovery (June 2024, around Father's Day):**
  - Daughter Nora Rowland drove from Maryland and learned from Smalls, over a Ring doorbell, that her father had died three days earlier.
  - The locks had been changed, the walls freshly painted, and there were unfamiliar photos inside.
  - Smalls called police. Officers handcuffed Nora after Smalls showed them the deed.
  - Nora's trespassing charges were later dismissed.
  - A neighbor had reported furniture being moved to senior services. (WSMV)
- **CBS Baltimore (WJZ), 9/3/2026** — https://www.cbsnews.com/baltimore/news/baltimore-woman-caregiver-stole-fathers-home-thousands-hid-death-south-carolina/
  - Nora alleges physical abuse and theft of about **$27,000**, including $14,000 in cash.
  - She says the home is worth **$300,000**, and that her father was moved to a facility without her being told.
  - Two years later, the caregiver still lives in the house and Nora is still paying the property taxes.
  - A civil complaint has been filed and a police investigation recently began.
  - AARP Maryland's Karen Morgan is on record with protective advice ("surprise calls").
- **Wealth of Geeks via Yahoo, 9/1/2026** — https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/caregiver-became-power-attorney-then-203239025.html
  - Griswold's initial denial was contradicted by subpoenaed documents.
  - Smalls told reporters to contact her attorney and called police on the news crew.
- **Griswold's statement:** Smalls was an independent caregiver on its registry who passed a background check, and she was no longer receiving services through Griswold when the power of attorney and deed were signed. (WSMV)
- **Status:** No criminal charges have been announced against Smalls. Conway police are investigating (WSMV; Yahoo).

### Second case to show it's a pattern (fresh arrest, 9/17/2026)
- **Port St. Lucie, FL:** Caregiver Barbara Ann Baumgarten, 60, was arrested **Sep 17** with help from the U.S. Marshals task force and held on a $310K bond.
- **The victim:** An 84-year-old man was found injured on his floor in March 2024. Detectives say:
  - his account fell from about **$105,971 to $4,897**;
  - she took out a **$616,550 mortgage** listing him as co-borrower without his knowledge.
- **Charges:** grand theft from a person 65+, exploitation of an elderly adult, and neglect. All are first-degree felonies.
- DCF removed him from her care and reunited him with family.
- Source: Wealth of Geeks via Yahoo, 9/21 — https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/elderly-man-found-injured-home-123036081.html

### Official peg for the protective turn (Sep 2026)

  - It recommends a voluntary **"title freeze, like a credit freeze,"** expedited quiet-title hearings, and stronger ID checks.
  - Coverage: NJ Monitor 9/10 https://newjerseymonitor.com/2026/09/10/watchdog-calls-for-nj-legislators-to-do-more-to-prevent-real-estate-fraud/ ; News 12 NJ 9/13 https://newjersey.news12.com/2026/09/13/nj-ranks-among-top-states-for-reported-deed-fraud-as-investigators-find-gaps-in-safeguards/393heas4bYhC9i9Z7NGBwh ; Jersey Vindicator 9/11 https://jerseyvindicator.org/2026/09/11/new-jersey-deed-fraud-is-rising-watchdog-urges-state-action

### Protective payoff (all from sources above)
- Do a **family check-in cadence plus surprise calls** (AARP MD, WJZ).
- **Watch for new POA and deed filings.** Sign up for your county recorder's property-fraud alert where one is offered (FBI and NJ SCI context).
- **Vet agency "independent contractors."** Griswold says Smalls was on its registry, not an employee.
- **Know the "title freeze" concept** (NJ SCI).
- **Unverified:** which counties or states actually offer recorder alerts. Pull this per market before air.

### Weaknesses and legal
- **Smalls is accused, not charged.** Use "allegedly" and "the daughter says." Consider not naming her or showing her face. The police investigation is open and the civil case is pending.
- **The underlying events are from 2024.** The freshness is the Aug 27–Sep 3, 2026 coverage wave plus the 9/17 FL arrest.
- **This is adjacent to pass-1 "home title theft,"** which was pitched but not aired. The new angle is the insider (caregiver plus POA), not a stranger forging a deed.
