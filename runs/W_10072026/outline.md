### The fix

- **Your alibi is on a timer.** Records cleared all three: Lipps with bank statements, Elser with her car's cameras. And police came months later: about two months for Lipps, about six for Isaacs.
- **Change auto-delete:** Google Maps → Your Timeline → ⋯ → Location & privacy settings → Auto-delete Timeline → 36 months, or off. *(verify on Jeff's phone)*
- **Turn on backup:** auto-delete must be off first. With backup on, your Timeline survives a lost or broken phone. Works on iPhone too, in the Google Maps app. *(verify)*
- **Know your other records:** how long your doorbell and dashcam keep footage, and that bank statements show where you were.
- Say it plainly: that history can clear you, and it can also be subpoenaed. Your call.

### The fix

- iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → each store app → Never. Then Settings → the app → Bluetooth off.
- Android: Settings → Apps → the store app → Permissions → Location → Don't allow. *(verify on Jeff's device)*
- With location and Bluetooth off, the basket tracks a basket, not you. The cameras and plate readers don't need your phone; those you can't switch off.
