**In one sentence:** The new AI grocery carts point cameras into your basket and out at the aisle, store baskets carry beacons that talk to the lights overhead, and home-store cameras log which aisles you walked and read your plate, but you can switch off the part that ties it to your phone.

| 2 | The beacon | Store worker who filmed it (Elijah, per his TikTok) | He films the black box on a store basket and calls it a tracker; 3.4 million people watch. | HAVE · VERTICAL — TikTok @user60342208753 |

| 4 | The fix | You (the viewer) | Jeff shuts off location and Bluetooth for store apps on his phone, live. | DEMO |

### The fix

- iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → each store app → Never. Then Settings → the app → Bluetooth off.
- Android: Settings → Apps → the store app → Permissions → Location → Don't allow. *(verify on Jeff's device)*
- With location and Bluetooth off, the basket tracks a basket, not you. The cameras and plate readers don't need your phone; those you can't switch off.
