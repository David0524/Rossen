### The fix
Android: Settings → Location → Location services → Bluetooth scanning off. Your phone keeps scanning for beacons even with Bluetooth "off" until you do this. Samsung is the same path.
Android, per app: Settings → Apps → the store app → Permissions → Nearby devices → Don't allow. (Security & privacy → Privacy → Permission manager shows every app that has it.)
iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → the store app → Never or While Using, and Precise Location off. Then Privacy & Security → Bluetooth → switch the store app off.
Opt out of the sharing: in the store app or site, "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" or "Your Privacy Choices."
With that off, the basket tracks a basket, not you. The cameras and AI carts don't need your phone; those you can't switch off.
